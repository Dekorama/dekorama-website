## Navigating Regulations and Permits for Holiday Rentals in Andalusia
Operating a holiday rental in Mijas, like anywhere in Andalusia, requires adherence to specific regional regulations. It is vital to understand these to avoid legal issues and ensure your property is compliant.

### Vivienda con Fines Turísticos (VFT) Registration
Your property must be registered with the Junta de Andalucía as a "Vivienda con Fines Turísticos" (VFT). This involves meeting certain technical requirements, such as having air conditioning in all bedrooms and living areas, a first aid kit, and a complaints book available to guests. The registration process is managed through the Registro de Turismo de Andalucía. You can find detailed information on the official Junta de Andalucía tourism website.

### Renovation Permits (Licencia de Obra)
Any renovation work, from minor changes to major structural reforms, requires a municipal building permit (licencia de obra) from the Mijas Town Hall. The type of permit needed depends on the scope of work:

*   **Licencia de Obra Menor**: For minor works like painting, tiling, or changing fixtures.
*   **Licencia de Obra Mayor**: For structural changes, extensions, or significant alterations to the property's layout. This typically requires an architect's project.

Failing to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines and even forced demolition of unauthorised work. A reputable renovation company will guide you through this process, ensuring all necessary documentation is submitted correctly.

Yes, all properties rented out for tourism purposes in Mijas, like elsewhere in Andalusia, must be registered as a "Vivienda con Fines Turísticos" (VFT) with the Junta de Andalucía. This ensures compliance with regional tourism regulations and avoids potential fines.
