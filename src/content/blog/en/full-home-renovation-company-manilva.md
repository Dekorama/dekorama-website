## How Much Do Full Home Renovations Cost in Manilva?
The cost of a full home renovation in Manilva varies significantly based on several factors. A general estimate for a mid-range renovation typically falls between €800 to €1,500 per square metre. However, luxury finishes or extensive structural changes can push costs higher.

Key factors influencing the overall price include:

*   **Property Size and Condition:** Larger properties or those requiring extensive repairs will naturally cost more.
*   **Material Quality:** The choice between standard, mid-range, or high-end finishes for flooring, kitchen units, bathroom fittings, and other materials significantly impacts the budget.
*   **Scope of Work:** Minor layout changes are less expensive than complete reconfigurations involving structural alterations.
*   **Permits and Fees:** Local council fees for building permits must be factored in.
*   **Labour Costs:** The expertise and number of tradespeople required influence labour expenses.

### Budgeting for Your Renovation
To help you budget effectively, consider the following approximate breakdown of costs:

*   **Demolition and Structural Work:** 15-20%
*   **Electrical and Plumbing Systems:** 15-20%
*   **Kitchen and Bathrooms:** 25-30% (these are often the most expensive rooms)
*   **Flooring and Wall Finishes:** 15-20%
*   **Windows, Doors, and Carpentry:** 10-15%
*   **Miscellaneous (permits, design fees, contingency):** 10-15%

Always allocate a contingency fund of 10-15% of your total budget for unforeseen issues that may arise during the project.

## How Long Does a Full Home Renovation Take in Manilva?
The timeline for a full home renovation in Manilva depends on the project's complexity, the property's size, and the efficiency of the renovation company. While every project is unique, here is a general guide:

*   **Small Apartment (50-70 sqm):** Expect 2 to 4 months. This typically involves updating a kitchen, bathroom, and general finishes without major structural changes.
*   **Medium Home (80-120 sqm):** A typical timeline is 3 to 6 months. This often includes significant layout changes, new kitchens and bathrooms, and comprehensive material upgrades.
*   **Large Villa (150+ sqm):** These extensive projects can take 5 to 9 months or even longer, especially if they involve adding extensions, swimming pools, or complex landscaping.

Factors that can influence the timeline include:

*   **Permit Acquisition:** Obtaining local permits can add several weeks or even months to the initial planning phase.
*   **Material Lead Times:** Specialised or imported materials may have longer delivery schedules.
*   **Design Complexity:** More intricate designs or custom-built elements require additional time for fabrication and installation.
*   **Unforeseen Issues:** Older properties often reveal unexpected challenges once demolition begins, which can extend the schedule.

A reputable company will provide a detailed project schedule at the outset, keeping you informed of progress and any potential delays.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a full renovation in Manilva?
Yes, a full home renovation in Manilva, especially one involving structural changes or layout modifications, requires a building permit (licencia de obra mayor) from the local town hall. Your renovation company should assist with this application process.

### How do I ensure quality with a renovation company?
Ensure quality by checking the company's portfolio, reading client testimonials, and verifying their professional accreditations. A clear contract detailing materials and finishes, along with regular site visits or progress reports, also helps maintain standards throughout the project.

### Can I live in my property during a full renovation?
Generally, it is not advisable to live in your property during a full renovation. The work involves significant dust, noise, and disruption, and essential services like water and electricity may be temporarily disconnected for safety and efficiency.

### What is the typical payment structure for renovations in Spain?
Payment structures typically involve an initial deposit (10-30%), followed by staged payments linked to project milestones (e.g., demolition complete, plumbing installed, finishes applied). A final payment is usually made upon satisfactory completion of all works.

### Is it better to buy new or renovate an older property in Manilva?
Renovating an older property in Manilva often allows for customisation to your exact tastes and can be more cost-effective than buying new, especially if the property is well-located.
