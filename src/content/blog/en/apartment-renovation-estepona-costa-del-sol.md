## How Much Does a Complete Apartment Renovation Cost in Estepona?
The cost of a complete apartment renovation in Estepona can vary significantly based on several factors. Understanding these elements will help you budget effectively for your project.

**Key Cost Factors:**

1.  **Apartment Size:** The larger the apartment, the higher the overall cost, though the per-square-metre rate might slightly decrease for larger projects due to economies of scale.
2.  **Scope of Work:** A renovation involving structural changes, full electrical rewiring, and new plumbing will be more expensive than one focused primarily on cosmetic updates.
3.  **Material Quality:** This is one of the biggest cost drivers. Choosing [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) for tiles, kitchen cabinets, bathroom fixtures, and flooring will naturally increase the budget compared to standard options.
4.  **Labour Costs:** Skilled tradespeople and experienced project managers are essential for a quality finish. Labour rates can vary, but investing in reputable professionals is always recommended.
5.  **Permits and Fees:** Local council permits and technical project fees are mandatory and add to the overall cost.

**Estimated Price Range per Square Metre:**

*   **Basic Renovation:** €600 - €800 per square metre. This typically includes standard quality materials, basic electrical and plumbing updates, and cosmetic finishes.
*   **Mid-Range Renovation:** €800 - €1,000 per square metre. This allows for better quality materials, more extensive system upgrades, and some customisation.
*   **High-End/Luxury Renovation:** €1,000 - €1,200+ per square metre. This involves premium materials, bespoke designs, advanced home automation, and significant structural alterations.

For a 90-square-metre apartment, a mid-range renovation could cost approximately €72,000 to €90,000. These figures are estimates, and a detailed quote from a reputable company like Dekorama is essential for an accurate budget.

## What is the Timeline for an Apartment Renovation in Estepona?
The duration of a complete apartment renovation in Estepona depends on the project's complexity, the size of the apartment, and the efficiency of the renovation team. Here is a typical breakdown:

1.  **Planning and Design (2-4 weeks):** This phase involves initial consultations, site visits, detailed design proposals, material selection, and finalising the project scope. It is crucial for setting clear expectations and avoiding delays later.

6.  **Plastering and Wall Preparation (2-3 weeks):** Preparing all surfaces for painting or tiling.

8.  **Kitchen and Bathroom Installation (3-5 weeks):** Fitting cabinets, sanitaryware, appliances, and countertops.
9.  **Finishing Touches (2-3 weeks):** Painting, installing doors, light fixtures, and final cleaning.

**Overall Project Duration:** For a standard apartment, a full renovation typically takes **2 to 5 months** from the start of demolition to completion. Larger or more complex projects can extend beyond this timeframe. Effective project management is key to staying on schedule.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Estepona
Understanding the local regulations and obtaining the correct permits is a critical step for any renovation project in Estepona. Failure to do so can lead to fines, project delays, or even the forced demolition of unauthorised work.

In Spain, renovation permits are generally categorised as 'Obra Menor' (minor works) or 'Obra Mayor' (major works).

*   **Obra Menor:** This applies to renovations that do not affect the structural integrity of the building, change the external appearance, or alter the use of the property. Examples include changing tiles, painting, or updating a kitchen without moving walls. While simpler, an 'Obra Menor' permit is still required from the Estepona Town Hall (Ayuntamiento de Estepona).
*   **Obra Mayor:** This is required for any renovation that involves structural changes, alterations to the facade, changes in the number of dwellings, or significant modifications to the layout that affect load-bearing walls. A technical project signed by an architect or technical architect is mandatory for 'Obra Mayor' permits.

Your chosen renovation company should be familiar with the specific requirements of the Estepona Town Hall and assist you in preparing and submitting all necessary documentation. This often includes architectural plans, technical reports, and proof of insurance. Engaging a professional team ensures compliance and a smooth approval process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an architect for my apartment renovation in Estepona?
For minor cosmetic renovations, an architect might not be strictly necessary. However, if your renovation involves structural changes, altering the layout significantly, or affecting load-bearing walls, a qualified architect or technical architect is legally required to draft and sign off on the project plans for permit approval.

### Can I live in my apartment during a full renovation?
Living in your apartment during a full renovation is generally not recommended. The process involves significant disruption, dust, noise, and often a lack of essential services like water or electricity. For safety and comfort, it is usually best to arrange alternative accommodation for the duration of the works.
