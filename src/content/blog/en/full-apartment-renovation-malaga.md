## What Does a Full Apartment Renovation Involve?
A "reforma integral" goes beyond cosmetic updates. It is a complete transformation of your living space. This type of renovation addresses every aspect of your apartment, ensuring a cohesive and functional design.

Key areas typically covered include:
*   **Demolition and Structural Work**: Removing old walls, reconfiguring layouts, and strengthening structures if necessary.
*   **Electrical and Plumbing Systems**: Updating outdated wiring, installing new fuse boxes, replacing old pipes, and relocating outlets or water points.
*   **Flooring**: Installing new flooring throughout, from elegant [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos) to modern laminate or wood.
*   **Kitchen Renovation**: Designing and installing [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida) with new cabinetry, countertops, appliances, and lighting.
*   **Bathroom Renovation**: Completely overhauling bathrooms, including new sanitaryware, tiling, showers, and fixtures for [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos).
*   **Interior and Exterior Carpentry**: Replacing doors, windows, and built-in wardrobes.
*   **Painting and Finishing**: Preparing surfaces, plastering, and applying fresh paint.
*   **Insulation and Soundproofing**: Improving thermal and acoustic comfort.

This comprehensive approach ensures that all elements work together seamlessly, creating a truly revitalised home.

## How Much Does a Full Apartment Renovation Cost in Malaga?
The cost of a full apartment renovation in Malaga varies significantly based on several factors. Expect to budget anywhere from €400 to €1,000 per square metre. This range accounts for different material qualities, the complexity of the work, and the size of your property.

Here is a breakdown of key cost drivers:

### Apartment Size and Layout
Larger apartments naturally incur higher costs due to more materials and labour. Complex layouts requiring significant structural changes or reconfigurations will also increase expenses.

### Quality of Materials
Your choice of finishes has a major impact on the final price.
*   **Standard Quality**: Basic tiles, laminate flooring, standard kitchen cabinets, and entry-level bathroom fixtures. Expect costs towards the lower end of the spectrum.
*   **Mid-Range Quality**: Higher quality [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) such as better-grade porcelain tiles, engineered wood flooring, custom-designed kitchens, and mid-range sanitaryware. This represents good value and durability.
*   **Luxury Quality**: High-end designer tiles, natural stone, bespoke carpentry, smart home technology, and premium brand appliances. These choices will place your project at the upper end of the cost scale.

### Scope of Work
A renovation that involves extensive demolition, re-piping, or rewiring will be more expensive than one focused primarily on cosmetic updates. Adding features like underfloor heating, smart home systems, or custom built-ins also contributes to the overall cost.

### Labour Costs
Skilled tradespeople in Malaga and across the Costa del Sol command competitive rates. The number of professionals involved and the duration of their work directly influence labour expenses.

### Permits and Fees
Local council permits (licencia de obra) are mandatory for most full renovations. These fees vary depending on the municipality and the scope of work. Always factor these administrative costs into your budget.

## How Long Does a Full Apartment Renovation Take?
The timeline for a full apartment renovation in Malaga depends on the project's complexity, the apartment's size, and the efficiency of the renovation team. Generally, you can expect a full renovation to take between 8 and 16 weeks.

Here is a typical breakdown of the stages and their approximate durations:

*   **Planning and Design (2-4 weeks)**: This initial phase involves consultations, architectural plans, material selection, and obtaining necessary permits. This is a crucial stage for defining your vision and budget.
*   **Demolition (1-2 weeks)**: Removing old fixtures, flooring, non-load-bearing walls, and preparing the space for new installations.
*   **Structural Work (if any) (1-3 weeks)**: Any changes to load-bearing walls or significant structural modifications.
*   **Rough-ins (Plumbing & Electrical) (2-3 weeks)**: Installing new pipes, wiring, and ductwork before walls are closed up.
*   **Plastering and Wall Preparation (1-2 weeks)**: Smoothing walls and ceilings, preparing surfaces for painting or tiling.
*   **Flooring Installation (1-2 weeks)**: Laying new tiles, wood, or other flooring materials.
*   **Kitchen and Bathroom Installation (2-4 weeks)**: Installing cabinets, countertops, sanitaryware, and fixtures.
*   **Finishing Touches (1-2 weeks)**: Painting, installing doors, skirting boards, lighting fixtures, and final clean-up.

Delays can occur due to unforeseen structural issues, material delivery times, or changes requested during the project. A reliable renovation company like Dekorama will provide a detailed schedule and keep you informed of progress.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Malaga
Understanding the local regulations is essential for any renovation project in Spain. For a full apartment renovation in Malaga, you will typically need a "licencia de obra mayor" (major works permit) or a "licencia de obra menor" (minor works permit) depending on the extent of the changes.

*   **Licencia de Obra Menor**: Required for simpler renovations that do not affect the structure, layout, or common elements of the building. Examples include changing flooring, painting, or updating a bathroom without altering its layout.
*   **Licencia de Obra Mayor**: Necessary for significant structural changes, alterations to the building's facade, changes in layout affecting load-bearing walls, or modifications to plumbing and electrical systems that require new plans. A qualified architect or technical surveyor (aparejador) must prepare the project plans for this type of permit.

The application process involves submitting detailed plans, budgets, and technical reports to the local town hall (Ayuntamiento de Málaga). It is advisable to work with a renovation company that has experience with local regulations and can manage the permit application on your behalf. This ensures compliance and avoids potential fines or project stoppages. You can find more information on local planning regulations through the official website of the Ayuntamiento de Málaga: [https://www.malaga.eu/](https://www.malaga.eu/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an architect for a full renovation?
For a "licencia de obra mayor" involving structural changes or significant layout modifications, an architect or technical surveyor is legally required to draw up the project plans and oversee the work. For minor cosmetic updates, an architect may not be necessary.

### Can I live in the apartment during the renovation?
It is generally not advisable to live in the apartment during a full renovation. The process involves significant dust, noise, and disruption, including periods without water or electricity. For safety and comfort, temporary alternative accommodation is recommended.
