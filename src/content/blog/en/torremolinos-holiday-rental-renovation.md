## What is the typical cost of renovating an apartment for holiday rental in Torremolinos?
The cost of renovating an apartment for holiday rental in Torremolinos varies widely based on several factors. Understanding these elements will help you budget effectively and make informed decisions.

### Factors Influencing Cost
*   **Size and layout of the apartment**: Larger properties or those requiring significant layout changes will naturally incur higher costs.
*   **Scope of work**: A cosmetic refresh (paint, minor upgrades) is far less expensive than a full structural overhaul or [full home renovations on the Costa del Sol](/en/reformas-integrales).
*   **Quality of materials**: Choosing high-end finishes like specific [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos) or bespoke fixtures will increase costs, but often adds significant value and durability.
*   **Labour costs**: These vary depending on the complexity of the work and the expertise of the renovation team.
*   **Permits and fees**: Local council permits are required for most renovation work, adding to the overall expense.

### Cost Breakdown Examples
*   **Basic Refresh (€15,000 - €25,000)**: This typically includes repainting, minor updates to kitchens and bathrooms (e.g., new taps, shower screens), new light fixtures, and basic furnishing. It is suitable for properties that are already in good condition but need a modern touch.
*   **Mid-Range Renovation (€25,000 - €40,000)**: This level often involves a new, functional kitchen, [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos), new flooring throughout, updated electricals, and potentially installing air conditioning. This significantly enhances comfort and appeal.
*   **Full Overhaul (€40,000+)**: For properties requiring extensive work, such as reconfiguring the internal layout, replacing all plumbing and electrical systems, installing new windows, and high-quality finishes. This level of renovation can transform an outdated property into a luxury holiday home.

## Key Upgrades to Maximise Rental Appeal and ROI
When renovating for holiday rentals, every upgrade should be considered for its ability to attract guests, withstand frequent use, and offer a strong return on investment. Focus on areas that guests value most.

### Kitchens and Bathrooms
These are often the most scrutinised areas by potential renters. Invest in modern, clean, and highly functional spaces. For kitchens, consider durable countertops, efficient appliances, and ample storage. For bathrooms, focus on contemporary fixtures, good lighting, and easy-to-clean surfaces. A well-designed [custom kitchen](/en/cocinas-a-medida) and stylish bathrooms can be major selling points.

### Flooring and Walls
Opt for durable, easy-to-maintain flooring that can withstand heavy foot traffic, such as high-quality porcelain tiles. Neutral, bright paint colours on walls create a sense of space and cleanliness, allowing guests to easily imagine themselves in the property. They also provide a versatile backdrop for furnishing and decor.

### Comfort and Convenience
Essential amenities significantly impact guest satisfaction and reviews. High-speed, reliable Wi-Fi is non-negotiable. Air conditioning is crucial for summer rentals on the Costa del Sol. Comfortable beds, quality linens, and sufficient storage space are also highly valued. Good lighting, both natural and artificial, enhances the overall ambience.

### Outdoor Spaces
If your apartment has a balcony or terrace, treat it as an extension of the living space. Furnish it with comfortable seating, a small dining area, and perhaps some plants. Outdoor spaces are a huge draw for holidaymakers in Torremolinos, offering a place to relax and enjoy the Mediterranean climate.
