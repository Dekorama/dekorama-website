## What Does a Full Villa Renovation Entail?
A 'reforma integral' for a villa goes far beyond cosmetic updates. It involves a complete overhaul of the property, often including structural modifications, layout reconfigurations, and the replacement of all major systems. The goal is to create a completely renewed living space that meets modern standards of comfort, efficiency, and design.

Key aspects typically include:

*   **Structural Changes:** Modifying internal walls, expanding living areas, or adding new extensions.
*   **System Upgrades:** Installing new electrical wiring, plumbing, heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems to improve efficiency and safety.
*   **Interior Finishes:** Replacing all flooring, wall coverings, ceilings, and internal doors. This often involves selecting high-quality materials such as porcelain tiles, natural stone, or engineered wood flooring.
*   **Kitchen and Bathrooms:** Complete redesign and installation of custom kitchens and complete bathroom renovations, featuring modern fixtures, fittings, and appliances.
*   **Exterior Enhancements:** Landscaping, pool renovation, terrace upgrades, and facade improvements to boost curb appeal and outdoor living spaces.
*   **Energy Efficiency:** Incorporating insulation, double-glazing, and renewable energy solutions to reduce utility costs and environmental impact.

### Cost Tiers Explained
To give you a clearer picture, renovation costs can be broadly categorised:

*   **Basic Renovation (€800-€1,100/sqm):** Focuses on updating aesthetics and functionality without major structural changes. This might include new flooring, painting, basic kitchen and bathroom updates, and minor electrical or plumbing revisions.
*   **Mid-Range Renovation (€1,100-€1,400/sqm):** Involves more significant upgrades, some layout modifications, and higher quality finishes. Expect new windows, improved insulation, updated electrical and plumbing systems, and modern custom kitchens and complete bathroom renovations.
*   **Luxury Renovation (€1,400-€1,800+/sqm):** A complete transformation with extensive structural work, high-end premium materials, bespoke features, smart home technology integration, and sophisticated design. ## What Factors Influence the Cost of a Villa Renovation?
Understanding the variables that impact your budget is crucial for effective planning:

*   **Size and Age of the Villa:** Larger villas naturally cost more to renovate. Older properties often require more extensive work on foundations, roofing, and outdated systems.
*   **Scope of Work:** A full gut renovation with structural changes will always be more expensive than a cosmetic refresh. The more elements you wish to change or upgrade, the higher the cost.
*   **Material Choices:** The selection of materials significantly affects the budget. Opting for premium materials like high-end porcelain tiles in Málaga, designer taps, or bespoke carpentry will increase costs compared to standard options.
*   **Structural Modifications:** Moving load-bearing walls, adding extensions, or altering the roofline requires engineering calculations and more intensive labour, driving up expenses.
*   **Permits and Fees:** Local council permits, known as *Licencia de Obra Mayor* for major works, involve fees. Architects' and engineers' fees for plans and project supervision are also necessary.
*   **Accessibility:** If your villa is difficult to access for construction vehicles or material delivery, labour costs might increase.
*   **Unexpected Issues:** Older properties can hide unforeseen problems like damp, pest infestations, or structural weaknesses, which can add to the budget once discovered.

## How Long Does a Villa Renovation Take in San Pedro de Alcántara?
The timeline for a full villa renovation in San Pedro de Alcántara can range from 6 to 18 months, depending on the project's complexity and size. It is important to factor in potential delays, especially concerning permits and material deliveries.

Here is a typical breakdown of the stages:

*   **Planning and Design (1-3 months):** This phase involves working with architects and designers to finalise plans, select materials, and obtain the necessary building permits from the Ayuntamiento de Marbella. This stage is critical for ensuring a smooth execution.
*   **Demolition and Structural Work (1-2 months):** Once permits are secured, the existing interior is stripped out, and any structural modifications, such as wall removals or new openings, are carried out. This is often the messiest part of the renovation.
*   **Installations (2-4 months):** New electrical wiring, plumbing, HVAC systems, and insulation are installed. This stage requires careful coordination among different trades.
*   **Finishing Touches (2-6 months):** This is where your villa truly starts to take shape. It includes plastering, painting, tiling, installation of new flooring, custom kitchens, complete bathroom renovations, internal doors, and built-in wardrobes. Exterior work like landscaping and pool renovation also occurs here.

For expats and international property owners, understanding the local regulations is paramount. A 'reforma integral' in San Pedro de Alcántara, being part of Marbella, requires a *Licencia de Obra Mayor* (Major Works Permit) from the Ayuntamiento de Marbella. This permit is essential for any work that affects the structure, layout, or exterior appearance of your villa.
