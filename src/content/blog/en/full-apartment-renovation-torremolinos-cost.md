## What is a full apartment renovation?

A full apartment renovation, known in Spanish as a "reforma integral de piso", involves a complete overhaul of your property. This goes beyond cosmetic updates, encompassing structural changes, reconfiguring layouts, updating electrical and plumbing systems, and replacing all finishes. It is a comprehensive transformation designed to modernise the space, improve functionality, and often increase property value.

For many properties in Torremolinos, especially older apartments, a full renovation addresses outdated infrastructure, improves energy efficiency, and brings the home up to contemporary standards. This includes everything from new flooring and wall finishes to complete overhauls of your kitchen and bathrooms.

## How much does a full apartment renovation cost in Torremolinos?

The cost of a full apartment renovation in Torremolinos varies significantly based on several factors, primarily the size of your apartment, the quality of materials chosen, and the complexity of the work involved. As a general guide, you can expect to pay:

*   **Basic Renovation**: €600 - €800 per square metre. This includes standard quality materials, functional updates, and essential replacements.
*   **Mid-Range Renovation**: €800 - €1,000 per square metre. Here, you will find better quality finishes, more design flexibility, and potentially some layout changes.
*   **High-End/Luxury Renovation**: €1,000 - €1,200+ per square metre. This category involves premium materials, bespoke designs, advanced home automation, and complex structural modifications.

These figures are estimates. Always obtain detailed quotes from several reputable companies to get an accurate understanding for your specific project.

### Key Cost Factors

Several elements contribute to the overall price of your renovation:

*   **Demolition and Waste Removal**: The initial phase involves tearing down existing structures and disposing of debris, which has associated labour and disposal costs.
*   **Structural Changes**: Moving walls, creating open-plan spaces, or reinforcing structures will add significant costs due to engineering requirements and increased labour.
*   **Plumbing and Electrical Systems**: Updating or replacing old pipework and wiring is crucial for safety and functionality, especially in older properties. This is a non-negotiable expense for a true "reforma integral".
*   **Kitchen and Bathroom Renovations**: These are typically the most expensive rooms due to the specialised fixtures, tiling, and installations required. Investing in a [custom kitchen](/en/cocinas-a-medida) or [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos) can greatly impact the budget.
*   **Flooring and Tiling**: The choice of flooring, such as [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos), wood, or microcement, significantly affects the budget. Installation costs also vary.
*   **Windows and Doors**: Replacing old windows with modern, energy-efficient units can improve insulation and soundproofing but comes at a cost.
*   **Finishes and Materials**: The quality of paint, plasterwork, built-in wardrobes, and other decorative elements directly influences the final price. Opting for [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) will elevate the cost but also the aesthetic and durability.
*   **Architect/Designer Fees**: If you hire an architect or interior designer for planning and oversight, their fees will be an additional expense.
*   **Permits and Licences**: Municipal fees for obtaining the necessary building permits are mandatory.

### Budgeting Tips

To manage your renovation budget effectively:

*   **Detailed Planning**: Have a clear vision and detailed plans before starting. Changes during construction are costly.
*   **Contingency Fund**: Always allocate 10-15% of your total budget for unexpected issues that may arise.
*   **Prioritise**: Decide which areas are most important to you and allocate your budget accordingly. You might splurge on a kitchen but save on guest bathroom finishes.
*   **Compare Quotes**: Obtain at least three detailed quotes from different reputable contractors. Ensure they specify material quality and labour costs.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Torremolinos

For a full apartment renovation in Torremolinos, you will almost certainly need a "licencia de obra mayor" (major works permit) from the local council, the Ayuntamiento de Torremolinos. This permit is required for any work that affects the structure, layout, or exterior appearance of the building, or involves significant changes to plumbing and electrical systems.

The process typically involves submitting detailed plans drawn up by a qualified architect or technical architect (aparejador), along with various forms and fees. It is advisable to engage a local professional or your chosen renovation company to handle this on your behalf, as they will be familiar with the specific requirements and bureaucratic procedures. Failure to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines, forced demolition, or difficulties when selling the property.

For up-to-date information on municipal regulations and permit applications, it is always best to consult the official website of the [Ayuntamiento de Torremolinos](https://www.torremolinos.es/) or visit their urban planning department directly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an architect for a full renovation in Torremolinos?
Yes, for a "licencia de obra mayor" involving structural changes or significant layout modifications, an architect or technical architect (aparejador) is legally required to draw up the plans and oversee the work. They ensure compliance with local building codes and safety regulations.

### Can I live in my apartment during a full renovation?
It is generally not advisable to live in your apartment during a full renovation, especially if it involves demolition, plumbing, or electrical work. The disruption, noise, dust, and lack of essential services make it impractical and unsafe. Plan for alternative accommodation.

### How do I choose materials for my renovation?
Choosing materials involves balancing aesthetics, durability, and budget. Visit showrooms, consult with your renovation company or designer, and consider the climate of Torremolinos. Dekorama offers a wide range of [premium materials] and expert advice to help you make informed decisions.

### What is the difference between a "reforma integral" and "reforma parcial"?
A "reforma integral" is a full, comprehensive renovation affecting the entire property, including structural and system changes. A "reforma parcial" is a partial renovation, focusing on specific areas or cosmetic updates, like painting a room or updating a single bathroom, without major structural impact.

### What are the payment terms for a renovation project?
Payment terms typically involve an initial deposit, followed by staged payments linked to project milestones (e.g., completion of demolition, plumbing, tiling). A final payment is usually made upon satisfactory completion of all works. Always ensure terms are clearly outlined in your contract.
