## What is a Full Apartment Renovation (Reforma Integral)?

A "reforma integral" goes far beyond cosmetic updates. It involves a complete overhaul of your apartment, often including structural changes, new layouts, and updated installations. This type of renovation is ideal for older properties in Manilva that require significant modernisation, or for owners looking to completely customise their living space.

The scope typically includes:

*   **Demolition and structural work:** Removing non-load-bearing walls, creating open-plan spaces.
*   **Electrical and plumbing systems:** Replacing old wiring and pipes to meet current safety standards and improve efficiency.
*   **Flooring:** Installing new flooring throughout, from elegant porcelain tiles to durable laminate or wood.
*   **Wall finishes:** Re-plastering, painting, and decorative finishes.
*   **Kitchen and bathroom remodelling:** Complete replacement of fixtures, fittings, and appliances, often involving custom kitchens and complete bathroom renovations.
*   **Doors and windows:** Upgrading for better insulation, security, and aesthetics.
*   **Lighting:** Designing and installing new lighting schemes.

Essentially, a full renovation allows you to reimagine your apartment, enhancing both its functionality and market value.

*   **Apartment Size:** Larger apartments naturally incur higher costs due to more materials and labour.
*   **Material Quality:** Choosing premium materials, such as high-end porcelain tiles in Málaga, designer taps, or bespoke kitchen units, will increase the overall budget. Conversely, standard-grade materials offer a more economical option.
*   **Scope of Work:** Extensive structural changes, moving plumbing or electrical points, or installing underfloor heating will add to the complexity and cost.

*   **Permits and Fees:** Local council permits (licencias de obra) are mandatory and come with associated fees.
*   **Design and Project Management:** Engaging an architect or interior designer adds to the cost but ensures a cohesive and professional outcome.

The timeline for a full apartment renovation in Manilva depends heavily on the project's scale and complexity. While minor cosmetic updates might take a few weeks, a comprehensive reforma integral typically requires a minimum of 8 to 16 weeks.

## Navigating the Renovation Process in Manilva

Undertaking a renovation in a foreign country can seem daunting, but with the right guidance, it can be a smooth experience. Here's what you need to know about the process in Manilva.

### 1. Permits and Regulations

All significant renovation work in Spain requires a municipal building permit (licencia de obra) from the local town hall (Ayuntamiento). For a full apartment renovation, you will typically need a *licencia de obra mayor* (major works permit), which involves submitting detailed plans drawn up by an architect. This ensures compliance with local urban planning regulations and safety standards.

It is advisable to engage a professional renovation company that can assist with permit applications. They will know the specific requirements of the Ayuntamiento de Manilva. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an architect for a full apartment renovation in Manilva?
Yes, for a full apartment renovation (reforma integral) in Manilva, you will almost certainly need an architect. They are required to draw up the plans and oversee the work for the *licencia de obra mayor* (major works permit) issued by the local Ayuntamiento. This ensures structural integrity and compliance with local building codes.

### Can I live in my apartment during a full renovation?
It is generally not advisable to live in your apartment during a full renovation. The process involves significant disruption, including dust, noise, lack of utilities (water, electricity), and safety hazards. Most clients arrange alternative accommodation for the duration of the project to ensure comfort and safety.

### What is the typical payment structure for a renovation project?
Payment structures usually involve an initial deposit (10-30%), followed by staged payments tied to project milestones (e.g., demolition complete, plumbing installed, kitchen fitted). A final payment is made upon satisfactory completion of all works. This phased approach protects both the client and the contractor.

### How do I ensure the quality of materials used?
Ensure your contract specifies the exact materials to be used, including brands and models where applicable. Visiting the contractor's showroom, like Dekorama's in Benalmádena, allows you to inspect premium materials firsthand. Reputable companies provide guarantees on both materials and workmanship, ensuring lasting quality.

### What guarantees do renovation companies offer?
Professional renovation companies in Spain typically offer guarantees on their workmanship, usually for one to three years, and sometimes longer for structural elements. Materials often come with separate manufacturer warranties. Always clarify the warranty terms in your contract before starting any work to ensure you are fully protected.
