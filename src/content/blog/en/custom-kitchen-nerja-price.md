## What Influences Custom Kitchen Costs in Nerja?
The price of a custom kitchen is not a single figure, but rather a spectrum influenced by several key decisions and elements. Understanding these factors will help you budget effectively and make informed choices for your Nerja home.

### Materials and Finishes
The choice of materials for cabinetry, countertops, splashbacks, and flooring significantly impacts the overall cost. For cabinets, options range from more economical laminates and MDF with various finishes to premium solid wood or lacquered doors. Countertops can vary from durable laminates and wood to high-end quartz, granite, marble, or sophisticated porcelain tiles in Málaga. Each material offers different aesthetic qualities, durability, and price points.

### Kitchen Size and Layout
Naturally, a larger kitchen requires more materials and labour, increasing the cost. The layout also plays a role. A simple galley kitchen will generally be less expensive than a sprawling U-shaped kitchen with a large island. Complex layouts that require extensive custom cabinetry, intricate plumbing, or electrical reconfigurations will also add to the budget.

### Appliances and Fixtures
Appliances can account for a substantial portion of your kitchen budget. Integrated appliances, high-end brands, and smart technology features will increase costs compared to standard freestanding models. Similarly, your choice of taps, sinks, and lighting fixtures, from basic options to designer pieces, will affect the final price. Consider energy efficiency ratings, as these can save money in the long run.

### Design Complexity and Customisation
The more bespoke and intricate your kitchen design, the higher the cost. This includes custom-built storage solutions, unique island designs, specialised lighting schemes, and features like wine coolers or integrated coffee machines. Highly customised elements require more detailed planning, skilled craftsmanship, and often more expensive materials.

### Installation and Labour
Professional installation is crucial for a custom kitchen. This includes demolition of the old kitchen, plumbing and electrical work, plastering, painting, tiling, and the precise fitting of cabinets and countertops. The complexity of the installation, site access, and the need for structural modifications will all contribute to labour costs. Engaging experienced professionals ensures a high-quality finish and compliance with local regulations.

## Permits and Regulations for Kitchen Renovations in Spain
Undertaking a kitchen renovation in Nerja, or anywhere in Spain, often requires local permits. The type of permit depends on the scope of work:

*   **Licencia de Obra Menor (Minor Works Permit):** This is typically required for non-structural changes like replacing cabinets, countertops, flooring, or retiling. It's a simpler process, but still necessary.

*   **Licencia de Obra Mayor (Major Works Permit):** If your renovation involves structural changes, significant alterations to the layout, or major plumbing and electrical reconfigurations, a major works permit is usually needed. This requires plans approved by an architect and more extensive documentation.

It is crucial to check with the Nerja *Ayuntamiento* (town hall) or a local architect/project manager before starting any work. Failing to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines and potential issues when selling your property. ## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a custom kitchen renovation take?
A custom kitchen renovation in Nerja typically takes between 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the complexity of the design, the extent of structural changes, and the lead time for custom materials. This timeframe includes design, material ordering, demolition, and installation.

### Do I need a permit for a kitchen renovation in Nerja?
Yes, most kitchen renovations in Nerja require a permit from the local *Ayuntamiento*. Minor changes like replacing cabinets might need a *Licencia de Obra Menor*, while structural alterations will require a *Licencia de Obra Mayor*. Always check local regulations before starting work.

### What's the difference between a custom kitchen and a modular one?
A custom kitchen is designed and built specifically for your space and preferences, offering unique dimensions and features. A modular kitchen uses pre-manufactured units that are assembled to fit, providing less flexibility in design and size but often at a lower cost.
