*   **Material Quality:** The choice of materials has a major impact. ## Understanding Renovation Timelines in Mijas
The duration of a full home renovation in Mijas varies significantly based on the project's complexity and scope. While a small cosmetic update might take a few weeks, a comprehensive overhaul requires more time.

*   **Planning and Design (4-8 weeks):** This initial phase involves consultations, design proposals, material selection, and finalising plans. It is crucial for setting the project's direction.
*   **Permit Acquisition (4-12 weeks):** Obtaining the necessary building permits from the Mijas Town Hall can be a lengthy process. This timeline is outside the contractor's direct control and depends on municipal processing times.
*   **Construction Phase (8-24 weeks):** This is where the physical work happens. Demolition, structural changes, new installations (electrical, plumbing), plastering, flooring, kitchen and bathroom fitting, and final painting all contribute to this phase. A typical full renovation for a 100m² property often takes 3 to 6 months of active construction.

Factors like unforeseen structural issues in older properties, changes requested during the project, or delays in material delivery can extend these timelines. Clear communication with your renovation company is vital to manage expectations.

## Permits and Regulations for Renovations in Mijas
Navigating local regulations is a critical step for any renovation project in Mijas. Failure to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines, project delays, or even the requirement to undo completed work.

In Mijas, as in other municipalities on the Costa del Sol, you will typically need a *Licencia de Obra Menor* (minor works permit) for cosmetic changes that do not affect the structure or layout, such as painting, new flooring, or updating a bathroom without changing its footprint. For more extensive work, such as structural modifications, changing the distribution of rooms, or adding extensions, a *Licencia de Obra Mayor* (major works permit) is required. This usually involves submitting architectural plans and a technical project by a qualified architect or engineer.

It is highly recommended to engage a local renovation company or architect who is familiar with the specific requirements of the Mijas Town Hall (*Ayuntamiento de Mijas*). They can guide you through the application process, ensuring all documentation is correct and submitted efficiently. You can find more information on local planning regulations on the official Mijas Town Hall website.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an architect for a full renovation in Mijas?
Yes, if your renovation involves structural changes, significant layout modifications, or an increase in living space, you will need an architect to draw up plans and oversee the project. For minor cosmetic updates, an architect may not be required.

### How can I save money on my Mijas renovation?
To save money, consider mid-range materials instead of luxury options, keep structural changes to a minimum, and reuse existing elements where possible. Obtaining multiple quotes and clearly defining your scope upfront also helps control costs.

### What is the typical payment schedule for a renovation?
Payment schedules usually involve an initial deposit (10-30%), followed by staged payments tied to project milestones (e.g., demolition complete, plumbing installed, finishes applied). A final payment is made upon satisfactory completion of the work.

### How long does it take to get renovation permits in Mijas?
Obtaining renovation permits (Licencia de Obra Mayor) in Mijas can take anywhere from 4 to 12 weeks, sometimes longer, depending on the complexity of the project and the current workload of the Mijas Town Hall's planning department. Minor permits are generally quicker.
