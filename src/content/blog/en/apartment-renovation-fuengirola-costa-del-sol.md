## What Does a Full Apartment Renovation (Reforma Integral) Include?
A full apartment renovation goes beyond superficial updates, addressing the core elements of your home to improve functionality, aesthetics, and energy efficiency. It is a holistic approach to transforming your living space.

### Key Areas of Focus
When we talk about a reforma integral, we are generally referring to a project that impacts multiple aspects of the apartment. This includes:

*   **Demolition and structural changes:** Removing non-load-bearing walls to create open-plan spaces or reconfigure layouts.
*   **Electrical systems:** Updating wiring, installing new sockets, switches, and modern lighting solutions to meet current safety standards and your needs.
*   **Plumbing systems:** Replacing old pipes, installing new fixtures in bathrooms and kitchens, and ensuring efficient water distribution.
*   **Flooring:** Laying new flooring throughout the apartment, from [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos-malaga) to wood or laminate.
*   **Walls and ceilings:** Plastering, painting, and potentially installing false ceilings for integrated lighting or improved insulation.
*   **Kitchen renovation:** A complete overhaul including new cabinetry, countertops, appliances, and layout design. Explore options for [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida).
*   **Bathroom renovation:** Modernising fixtures, tiling, showers, and baths. Consider a [complete bathroom renovation](/en/banos-completos) for a fresh look.
*   **Carpentry:** Replacing interior doors, wardrobes, and potentially exterior windows for better insulation and security.
*   **Insulation:** Improving thermal and acoustic insulation for greater comfort and energy savings.

### Structural vs. Cosmetic
Understanding the difference between structural and cosmetic changes is vital. Cosmetic updates involve painting, new flooring, or minor fixture replacements. Structural changes, however, involve altering load-bearing elements, reconfiguring room layouts, or significantly modifying electrical and plumbing systems. These require more extensive planning, specific permits, and expert execution to ensure safety and compliance with local building codes in Fuengirola.

## How Much Does a Full Apartment Renovation Cost in Fuengirola?
The cost of a full apartment renovation in Fuengirola varies widely, influenced by several factors. It is essential to get a clear understanding of these elements to budget effectively.

### Cost Factors
Several key elements drive the overall price of your renovation:

*   **Size of the apartment:** Larger apartments naturally require more materials and labour.
*   **Scope of work:** A complete overhaul with structural changes will cost more than a renovation focused on updating finishes.
*   **Quality of materials:** Choosing [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) like high-end porcelain, bespoke cabinetry, or designer taps will increase costs compared to standard options.
*   **Condition of the existing property:** Older properties often require more extensive work on plumbing, electrical, and insulation systems.
*   **Permits and fees:** Local council permits in Fuengirola come with associated fees.
*   **Professional fees:** Architects, interior designers, and project management services add to the overall cost.

### Price Ranges per Square Metre
As a general guideline for Fuengirola and the wider Costa del Sol, you can expect the following per square metre costs for a full apartment renovation:

*   **Basic renovation:** €400 - €600 per square metre. This includes standard quality materials, basic electrical and plumbing updates, and painting.
*   **Mid-range renovation:** €600 - €800 per square metre. This allows for better quality materials, more extensive changes to layouts, and improved finishes.
*   **High-end/Luxury renovation:** €800 - €1,000+ per square metre. This involves premium materials, custom designs, advanced home automation, and significant structural alterations.

For example, a 100-square-metre apartment could cost anywhere from €40,000 to €100,000 or more for a full renovation.

### Budgeting for the Unexpected
It is always wise to allocate an additional 10% to 15% of your total renovation budget for unforeseen issues. Older properties, especially, can hide surprises like deteriorated pipes, dampness, or outdated electrical systems that only become apparent once demolition begins. A contingency fund prevents delays and unexpected financial strain.

## Permits and Regulations for Renovations in Fuengirola
Navigating local regulations and obtaining the correct permits is a critical step for any renovation in Spain. For expats, this can be particularly challenging without local knowledge.

### Licencia de Obra Menor vs. Mayor
In Fuengirola, as in the rest of Spain, renovations generally fall into two categories for permitting purposes:

*   **Licencia de Obra Menor (Minor Works Permit):** Required for non-structural changes like painting, tiling, replacing kitchen or bathroom fixtures, or minor electrical updates. This permit is simpler to obtain and has lower fees.
*   **Licencia de Obra Mayor (Major Works Permit):** Necessary for structural changes, significant layout alterations, facade modifications, or any work affecting the building's stability or common elements. This permit requires an architect's project, more detailed documentation, and a longer approval process.

It is crucial to determine which permit category your renovation falls into. Failing to obtain the correct permit can lead to fines, forced demolition, or difficulties when selling the property.

### Working with Your Contractor

They can prepare the necessary technical documentation and submit it on your behalf, saving you time and potential headaches. Always ensure your chosen contractor handles this aspect professionally.

### Do I need an architect for a full renovation?
Yes, if your full renovation involves structural changes, significant layout modifications, or affects the building's facade, a qualified architect's project and supervision are legally required for a Licencia de Obra Mayor in Fuengirola. For minor works, an architect may not be strictly necessary, but their expertise is always beneficial.

### Can I live in the apartment during renovation?
It is generally not advisable to live in the apartment during a full renovation. The process involves dust, noise, lack of essential services like water or electricity, and safety hazards. Most clients find alternative accommodation for the duration of the works for comfort and safety.

### What's the difference between partial and full renovation?
A partial renovation focuses on specific areas or elements, such as updating a single bathroom or kitchen, or just repainting. A full renovation, or reforma integral, involves a comprehensive overhaul of the entire apartment, often including structural changes, new electrical and plumbing systems, and complete redesigns of multiple spaces.
