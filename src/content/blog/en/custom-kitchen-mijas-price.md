## Factors Influencing Custom Kitchen Prices

Several key elements directly impact the final price of your custom kitchen. Understanding these will help you prioritise your budget and make informed decisions.

### Materials and Finishes

The choice of materials for your cabinetry, worktops, and splashbacks is one of the most significant cost drivers. Laminate or melamine cabinets offer a more economical option, while lacquered MDF, solid wood, or bespoke veneers represent a higher investment. Similarly, worktop materials range from affordable laminates to mid-range quartz and high-end natural stone like granite or marble.

For flooring and wall coverings, options like durable [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos) are popular for their aesthetic appeal and longevity. The quality and origin of these [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) directly correlate with their price, impacting the overall budget for your custom kitchen.

### Size and Layout

The physical dimensions of your kitchen space and its layout play a crucial role. A larger kitchen naturally requires more materials and labour. A complex layout, such as an L-shape or U-shape with many corners, or an island unit, will involve more intricate design and installation work compared to a simple galley kitchen. Open-plan designs that integrate with living areas might also require additional considerations for seamless transitions and aesthetic continuity.

### Appliances and Fixtures

Appliances can account for a substantial portion of your kitchen budget. Basic freestanding models are more affordable, while integrated, high-specification appliances from premium brands, such as induction hobs, pyrolytic ovens, smart refrigerators, and wine coolers, will significantly increase the cost. Similarly, the choice of taps, sinks, and other fixtures, from standard chrome to designer brassware or boiling water taps, will affect the overall price.

### Installation and Labour

The expertise required for installing a custom kitchen is considerable. This includes not only fitting the cabinets and worktops but also plumbing, electrical work, tiling, and potentially structural modifications. The cost of labour varies based on the complexity of the design and the experience of the installation team. Opting for a full-service company like Dekorama, which manages all aspects from design to installation, often provides better value and peace of mind, ensuring a cohesive and professional finish.

### Design Complexity and Custom Features

A truly custom kitchen allows for unique features tailored to your lifestyle. This might include bespoke storage solutions, integrated lighting, hidden pantries, custom-sized units, or specific ergonomic considerations. While these features enhance functionality and aesthetics, they also add to the design and manufacturing costs. The more intricate and personalised the design, the higher the investment will be.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a custom kitchen renovation take?
Typically, a custom kitchen renovation takes between 6 to 12 weeks from initial design approval to final installation. This timeframe includes design, material ordering, manufacturing of bespoke elements, and the installation process. Complex designs or unforeseen structural issues can extend this period slightly.

### Do I need permits for a kitchen renovation in Mijas?
Minor cosmetic kitchen renovations, like replacing cabinets or worktops, usually do not require a major permit. However, if you are altering the layout, moving plumbing or electrical points, or making structural changes, you will likely need a minor works permit (licencia de obra menor) from the Mijas town hall. Always check local regulations.

### What's the difference between a modular and a custom kitchen?
A modular kitchen uses pre-manufactured, standard-sized units that are assembled to fit your space, offering less flexibility in design. A custom kitchen is designed and built specifically for your unique space and preferences, allowing for bespoke sizes, shapes, materials, and features for a truly personalised result.

### Can I use my existing appliances in a new custom kitchen?
Yes, it is often possible to integrate your existing appliances into a new custom kitchen design, provided they are in good working order and fit the new layout. Our designers can assess your current appliances and incorporate them into the plans, helping you save on costs where appropriate.

### What payment terms are typical for a kitchen renovation?
Standard payment terms usually involve an initial deposit to commence design and material ordering, followed by staged payments at key project milestones, such as material delivery and installation commencement.
