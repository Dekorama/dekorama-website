## How Much Does a Complete Bathroom Renovation Cost in Nerja?
The cost of a complete bathroom renovation in Nerja is not a fixed figure. It varies widely based on several key elements, including the size of your bathroom, the quality of materials chosen, the extent of structural changes, and the labour involved. For a standard 6-8 square metre bathroom, you can anticipate costs falling within the €6,000 to €15,000 bracket.

This range accounts for everything from basic, functional upgrades to high-end, designer finishes. Understanding these contributing factors will help you budget accurately for your 2026 renovation project.

### Factors Influencing Bathroom Renovation Costs
Several variables directly impact the final price tag of your bathroom renovation. Demolition and waste removal are initial costs, followed by plumbing and electrical work, which can be significant if layouts are changed. Tiling, plastering, painting, and the installation of new fixtures all add to the total.

Accessibility modifications, such as walk-in showers or grab bars, also influence the budget. The age of your property might mean unforeseen issues, like outdated pipework or electrical systems, which can add to the complexity and cost.

### Material Choices and Their Impact
Materials are a major cost driver. Opting for standard ceramic tiles, basic sanitaryware, and chrome fittings will keep costs lower. However, choosing [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) such as large-format [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos), natural stone, designer taps, or custom-built vanity units will increase the investment.

Consider the long-term benefits of quality materials. They offer greater durability, require less maintenance, and provide a more luxurious feel. Dekorama's showroom in Benalmádena offers a wide selection of materials to suit various budgets and aesthetic preferences.

Labour costs are a significant component of any renovation. This includes the fees for plumbers, electricians, tilers, and general builders. Engaging a reputable company like Dekorama ensures you work with experienced, qualified professionals who adhere to local building standards.

### Permit and Regulatory Fees
Before starting any major renovation in Nerja, you will need to obtain the necessary permits from the local council (Ayuntamiento de Nerja). These permits ensure your renovation complies with local building codes and safety regulations. The cost of permits varies based on the scope of work.
