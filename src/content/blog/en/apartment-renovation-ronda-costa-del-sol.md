## How Much Does a Full Apartment Renovation Cost in Ronda?
The cost of a full apartment renovation in Ronda varies significantly based on several factors. Expect to budget between €600 and €1,200 per square metre for a comprehensive overhaul. This range accounts for different levels of finishes and the extent of structural changes.

Key cost drivers include:

*   **Material Quality:** Basic finishes like standard ceramic tiles and laminate flooring will be at the lower end. Opting for [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) such as high-end [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos), natural stone, or bespoke carpentry will increase costs considerably.
*   **Scope of Work:** A complete gut renovation involving new plumbing, electrical systems, and layout changes is more expensive than a cosmetic refresh. Moving walls, reconfiguring rooms, or adding new bathrooms are significant cost factors.
*   **Kitchen and Bathroom Renovations:** These areas are typically the most expensive per square metre due to specialised fittings, tiling, and cabinetry. A [custom kitchen](/en/cocinas-a-medida) or a [complete bathroom renovation](/en/banos-completos) can account for a large portion of the overall budget.

*   **Permits and Fees:** Local council permits (licencias de obra) are mandatory and incur fees, which vary based on the project's size and complexity. Architectural or technical project fees are also part of this.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Ronda
Understanding local permits and regulations is crucial for any renovation project in Spain, especially for expats. In Ronda, as elsewhere, you will need a *licencia de obra* (building permit) from the local town hall (Ayuntamiento de Ronda) before any work begins.

There are generally two types of permits:

*   **Licencia de Obra Menor (Minor Works Permit):** Required for smaller, non-structural renovations such as changing flooring, painting, or updating a bathroom without altering its layout. The process is simpler and quicker.
*   **Licencia de Obra Mayor (Major Works Permit):** Essential for a *reforma integral* that involves structural changes, altering the layout of the apartment, or affecting the building's facade. This permit requires a technical project signed by a qualified architect or technical engineer and is more complex to obtain.

It is highly advisable to work with a renovation company that has experience dealing with local authorities in the Málaga province. They can guide you through the application process, prepare the necessary documentation, and ensure your project complies with all municipal planning laws and building codes. Failure to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines, work stoppages, and even the obligation to reverse the changes.

A well-structured process ensures your full apartment renovation in Ronda proceeds smoothly and efficiently. Here is a typical breakdown of the steps involved:

1.  **Initial Consultation and Design:** This is where your vision takes shape. You discuss your ideas, needs, and budget with the renovation team. An architect or designer may create plans, 3D renderings, and material specifications.
2.  **Budgeting and Material Selection:** Based on the design, a detailed quote is prepared. You select all finishes, fixtures, and fittings. Dekorama's showroom in Benalmádena offers a wide range of [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium), including [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos), taps, and lighting, to help you make informed choices.
3.  **Permit Application:** Once designs and materials are finalised, the necessary *licencia de obra* is applied for at the Ayuntamiento de Ronda. This step can take several weeks or even months for a major works permit.
4.  **Demolition and Preparation:** Once permits are granted, the existing apartment is stripped back. Old fixtures, flooring, and non-load-bearing walls are removed. The site is prepared for new installations.
5.  **Structural, Plumbing, and Electrical Work:** This phase involves any structural modifications, installing new plumbing pipes, and updating the electrical system. This is the foundation for the rest of the renovation.
6.  **Installation of Major Elements:** New windows and doors are fitted. [Custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida) and [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos) begin, with tiling and fitting of sanitary ware.
7.  **Finishing Touches:** Flooring is laid, walls are plastered and painted, and interior carpentry (doors, wardrobes) is installed. Lighting fixtures and final electrical fittings are put in place.
8.  **Final Inspections and Handover:** The renovation team conducts a thorough quality check. You walk through the completed apartment, address any minor adjustments, and receive the keys to your newly transformed home.

This entire process for a full renovation typically takes between 8 and 16 weeks, depending on the apartment's size and the project's complexity.

### Do I need an architect for a full renovation in Ronda?
Yes, for a *reforma integral* involving structural changes, layout modifications, or affecting the building's facade, a qualified architect or technical engineer must sign off on the project plans. This is a legal requirement for obtaining a *licencia de obra mayor* from the Ayuntamiento de Ronda.

### How long does a full apartment renovation typically take?
A full apartment renovation in Ronda generally takes between 8 to 16 weeks. The exact duration depends on the apartment's size, the complexity of the design, the extent of structural work, and the efficiency of permit processing by the local council.

### Can I live in the apartment during the renovation?
It is generally not advisable to live in the apartment during a full renovation. The work involves significant dust, noise, and disruption, including periods without water or electricity. For safety and comfort, it is best to arrange alternative accommodation.
