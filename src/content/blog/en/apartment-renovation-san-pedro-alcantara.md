## How Much Does a Full Apartment Renovation Cost in San Pedro de Alcántara?
The cost of a full apartment renovation, or 'reforma integral', in San Pedro de Alcántara varies significantly based on several factors. As a general guideline, you can expect to pay anywhere from €800 to €1,500+ per square metre. This range accounts for standard to high-end finishes and comprehensive structural work.

### Factors Influencing Cost
Several elements contribute to the final price tag of your renovation project:

*   **Size and Layout:** Larger apartments naturally incur higher costs. Complex layouts requiring significant reconfigurations also increase labour and material expenses.
*   **Scope of Work:** A cosmetic update (painting, new flooring) is far less expensive than a full overhaul involving new plumbing, electrical systems, and structural changes.
*   **Material Choices:** Premium materials like high-end [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos), bespoke cabinetry for [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida), or designer bathroom fittings will elevate costs. Opting for standard, good quality materials keeps the budget more manageable.
*   **Labour Costs:** Skilled tradespeople on the Costa del Sol command fair wages. The complexity and duration of the work directly impact labour expenses.
*   **Permits and Fees:** All significant renovation work in Spain requires permits. In San Pedro de Alcántara, falling under the Marbella Town Hall, you will need a 'licencia de obra menor' for minor works or a 'licencia de obra mayor' for structural changes or layout modifications. These permits come with associated fees and require technical projects from an architect or technical engineer.
*   **Professional Fees:** Architects, interior designers, and project managers add value through expertise and oversight, but their fees are an additional cost.

### Cost Breakdown Example
While every project is unique, a typical 'reforma integral' might see costs distributed as follows (percentages are approximate):

*   **Demolition and Waste Removal:** 5-10%
*   **Structural Work (if any):** 10-20%
*   **Plumbing and Electrics:** 15-25%
*   **Flooring and Tiling:** 10-20%
*   **Kitchen Installation:** 10-15%
*   **Bathroom Installations:** 10-15% (for each bathroom)
*   **Painting and Finishing:** 5-10%
*   **Windows and Doors:** 5-10%
*   **Permits and Professional Fees:** 5-10%

## What Does a 'Reforma Integral' Involve?
A 'reforma integral' means a complete renovation, transforming an apartment from top to bottom. It is a comprehensive process designed to modernise, improve functionality, and enhance the aesthetic appeal of your property.

### Key Areas of Renovation

*   **Kitchen Remodel:** This often involves new cabinetry, countertops, appliances, plumbing, and electrical outlets. A [custom kitchen](/en/cocinas-a-medida) can significantly boost property value and living comfort.
*   **Bathroom Renovation:** Updating fixtures, tiling, showers, and toilets, or even reconfiguring the layout for a more spacious feel. [Complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos) are a popular choice for modernising older properties.
*   **Flooring:** Replacing old tiles or laminate with new options like wood, microcement, or [porcelain tiles](/en/porcelanicos) for a fresh look and improved durability.
*   **Electrical Systems:** Upgrading wiring, adding new sockets, installing modern lighting, and ensuring compliance with current Spanish regulations (e.g., *Reglamento Electrotécnico para Baja Tensión*).
*   **Plumbing Systems:** Replacing old pipes, updating water heaters, and ensuring efficient water distribution throughout the apartment.
*   **Layout Changes:** Moving non-load-bearing walls to create open-plan living areas or reconfiguring rooms for better flow and functionality.
*   **Windows and Doors:** Installing new, energy-efficient windows and modern interior or exterior doors for improved insulation, security, and aesthetics.
*   **Painting and Finishing:** Preparing walls, plastering, and applying fresh paint, along with installing skirting boards and other decorative elements.

## The Renovation Process: Step-by-Step
Understanding the typical stages of a 'reforma integral' helps you manage expectations and track progress.

### Initial Consultation and Design
The process begins with a consultation to discuss your vision, needs, and budget. Our team at Dekorama will visit your San Pedro de Alcántara apartment, take measurements, and develop a preliminary design concept. This stage often involves selecting [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) and finishes.

### Permitting and Planning
Once the design is finalised, the renovation company or your architect will prepare the technical project and submit it to the Marbella Town Hall for the required building permit. This stage can take several weeks or even months, depending on the complexity of the project and the local authority's processing times.

### Demolition and Structural Work
With permits in hand, demolition begins. Old fixtures, tiles, and non-load-bearing walls are removed. If structural changes are part of the plan, these are executed at this stage under strict supervision.

### Installations
This phase involves installing new electrical wiring, plumbing systems, and potentially heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) units. It is crucial for these 'hidden' elements to be installed correctly to avoid future problems.

### Finishes
Once the core installations are complete, the finishing work starts. This includes plastering walls, laying new flooring, tiling bathrooms and kitchens, installing new windows and doors, and painting. This is where your chosen aesthetics truly come to life.

### Final Touches and Handover
The final stage involves installing light fittings, bathroom fixtures, kitchen appliances, and any custom cabinetry. A thorough clean-up is performed, followed by a final inspection with you to ensure everything meets your expectations and our high standards. We then officially hand over your newly renovated apartment.

## How Long Does an Apartment Renovation Take in San Pedro de Alcántara?
The duration of an apartment renovation in San Pedro de Alcántara varies widely, typically ranging from 2 to 6 months for a full 'reforma integral'. Smaller, more cosmetic projects might be completed faster, while extensive overhauls can take longer.

### Typical Timelines

*   **Small Apartment (e.g., 60-80 sqm):** 2-4 months
*   **Medium Apartment (e.g., 80-120 sqm):** 3-5 months
*   **Large Apartment (e.g., 120+ sqm or complex layout):** 4-6+ months

These timelines include the actual construction work but do not always account for the permit acquisition period, which can add several weeks or months before work even begins.

### Factors Affecting Duration

*   **Permit Processing:** As mentioned, obtaining permits from the Marbella Town Hall can be a lengthy process.
*   **Material Availability:** Delays can occur if specific materials or custom-made items are on backorder or have long lead times.
*   **Scope and Complexity:** More extensive structural changes, bespoke joinery, or intricate designs naturally require more time.
*   **Unforeseen Issues:** Older properties, especially, can reveal unexpected problems once demolition begins (e.g., hidden damp, outdated wiring) that require additional work and time.
*   **Client Decisions:** Timely decision-making on material choices and design elements helps keep the project on schedule.
