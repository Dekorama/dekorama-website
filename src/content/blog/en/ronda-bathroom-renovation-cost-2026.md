## What is the Typical Timeline for a Bathroom Renovation in Ronda?

The duration of a complete bathroom renovation in Ronda typically ranges from 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the scope and complexity of the project. This timeline can be broken down into several key phases:

*   **Planning and Design (1-2 weeks, often overlapping):** This initial stage involves consultations, material selection, layout planning, and obtaining necessary permits. Working with a company that handles design and project management streamlines this process.
*   **Demolition (2-3 days):** Removing old fixtures, tiles, and potentially non-load-bearing walls. This is often the messiest part of the renovation.
*   **Plumbing and Electrical Work (3-5 days):** Installation or relocation of pipes, drains, and electrical outlets. This phase is critical and requires qualified professionals to ensure safety and compliance.
*   **Wall and Floor Preparation (2-4 days):** Preparing surfaces for tiling, including plastering, levelling, and waterproofing. Proper preparation is vital for a durable finish.
*   **Tiling (4-7 days):** Laying new floor and wall tiles. The time taken depends on the size of the bathroom, the type of tiles (e.g., large format tiles can take longer), and the complexity of the pattern.
*   **Fixture Installation (3-5 days):** Installing the shower tray, shower screen, toilet, vanity unit, basin, and taps. This also includes connecting all plumbing and electrical components.
*   **Finishing Touches (2-3 days):** Painting, installing lighting, mirrors, accessories, and final cleaning. This stage brings the entire project together.

Unexpected issues, such as discovering hidden plumbing problems or structural damage, can extend these timelines. A professional renovation company will communicate any potential delays transparently.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Ronda

Undertaking a complete bathroom renovation in Ronda, like anywhere else in Spain, requires adherence to local building regulations and obtaining the necessary permits. This is a crucial step to ensure your renovation is legal, safe, and compliant with municipal standards.

Most bathroom renovations will require a *licencia de obra menor* (minor works permit). This permit is for non-structural changes that do not alter the building's footprint or structural integrity. If your renovation involves significant structural alterations, such as moving load-bearing walls or major changes to the building's exterior, a *licencia de obra mayor* (major works permit) would be necessary, which involves a more complex application process and typically requires an architect's involvement.

It is essential to consult with the Ronda Town Hall (Ayuntamiento de Ronda) or a local architect/technical expert to understand the specific requirements for your project. Failing to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines, forced demolition, or difficulties when selling your property. Reputable renovation companies, such as Dekorama, often assist clients with the permit application process, leveraging their local knowledge and experience to ensure all paperwork is correctly submitted.
