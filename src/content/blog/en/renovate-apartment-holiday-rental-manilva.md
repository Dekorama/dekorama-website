## How Much Does it Cost to Renovate an Apartment for Holiday Rental in Manilva?

The cost of renovating an apartment for holiday rental in Manilva varies widely based on the size of the property, the extent of the renovation, and the quality of materials chosen. Here is a general breakdown of potential costs:

### Basic Refresh (Light Renovation)

This involves cosmetic updates to brighten and modernise the space without structural changes. Expect to pay **€15,000 to €25,000** for a typical 2-bedroom apartment. Work includes fresh paint throughout, minor repairs, updating light fixtures, new soft furnishings, and possibly a kitchen or bathroom facelift (e.g., new cabinet doors, regrouting tiles).

### Mid-Range Update (Partial Renovation)

This level of renovation focuses on key areas like the kitchen and bathrooms, along with general aesthetic improvements. Costs typically range from **€25,000 to €35,000**. This might include a new, functional kitchen, updated bathrooms with modern fittings, new flooring in key areas like porcelain tiles in Málaga, and a complete repaint. This offers a significant uplift in guest appeal.

### Full Overhaul (Comprehensive Renovation)

For older properties or those requiring a complete transformation, a full overhaul involves significant changes. This can include reconfiguring layouts, updating electrical and plumbing systems, installing new custom kitchens and complete bathroom renovations, and high-quality finishes throughout. Expect costs from **€35,000 to €45,000 or more**, depending on the size and complexity. This level of renovation ensures your property meets the highest standards for discerning guests.

Remember, these figures are estimates. Obtaining detailed quotes from experienced local renovation companies like Dekorama, based in Benalmádena, is essential for accurate budgeting. They can provide transparent pricing for premium materials and full home renovations on the Costa del Sol.

## How Long Does a Holiday Rental Renovation Take in Manilva?

The timeline for renovating an apartment for holiday rental in Manilva depends heavily on the scope of work. Planning and execution by experienced professionals can significantly streamline the process.

*   **Basic Refresh:** A light renovation, focusing on painting and minor updates, can typically be completed within **4 to 6 weeks**.
*   **Mid-Range Update:** Projects involving new kitchens, bathrooms, and flooring usually take around **6 to 10 weeks**.
*   **Full Overhaul:** A comprehensive renovation, including structural changes, new installations, and high-end finishes, can extend to **10 to 12 weeks or more**.

These timelines account for design, material sourcing, demolition, construction, and final touches. Unexpected issues can arise, so it is always wise to factor in a buffer period. Working with a full-service renovation company helps manage these complexities efficiently.

## Key Renovation Areas for Maximising Rental Appeal

Focusing your renovation efforts on specific areas yields the best return for holiday rentals. Guests often prioritise comfort, aesthetics, and functionality.

### Kitchens: The Heart of the Home

Modern, well-equipped kitchens are a major draw. Guests appreciate the option to cook their own meals. Consider durable, easy-to-clean surfaces, good quality appliances, and ample storage. Custom kitchens designed for holiday rental use can withstand frequent use while remaining stylish.

### Bathrooms: Spa-like Retreats

Clean, contemporary bathrooms are essential. Focus on high-quality taps and fittings, spacious shower trays, and elegant shower screens. Good lighting and ventilation are also important. Investing in complete bathroom renovations can transform a functional space into a luxurious one, enhancing the overall guest experience.

### Flooring: Durability Meets Style

Choose flooring that is both attractive and highly durable. Porcelain tiles are an excellent choice for holiday rentals on the Costa del Sol due to their resistance to wear, moisture, and ease of cleaning. They also offer a cool surface, which is ideal for the Spanish climate.

### Lighting and Ambiance

Effective lighting can transform a space. Use a mix of ambient, task, and accent lighting to create a welcoming atmosphere. Dimmers can add flexibility, allowing guests to adjust the mood. Consider modern, energy-efficient LED options.

### Outdoor Spaces: Balconies and Terraces

For properties in Manilva, outdoor spaces are highly valued. Invest in comfortable outdoor furniture, attractive outdoor materials, and perhaps some potted plants to create an inviting area for relaxation and dining. These spaces significantly enhance a property's appeal.
