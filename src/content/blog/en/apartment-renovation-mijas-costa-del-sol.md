## What is a 'Reforma Integral' and Why Choose One in Mijas?

A 'reforma integral' translates to a full or comprehensive renovation. It means going beyond cosmetic changes to completely transform your apartment. This often involves structural alterations, updating electrical and plumbing systems, replacing flooring, renovating kitchens and bathrooms, and reconfiguring layouts to better suit modern living.

Choosing a full renovation in Mijas offers numerous benefits, especially for expats and international property owners. A modernised apartment stands out in the rental market, commands higher resale values, and provides a more comfortable, energy-efficient home for personal use. Many older properties in Mijas can benefit greatly from updated insulation, new windows, and efficient air conditioning systems, which are crucial for the Costa del Sol climate.

## How Much Does an Apartment Renovation Cost in Mijas?

The cost of an apartment renovation in Mijas varies widely based on several factors. It is not a one-size-fits-all figure, but understanding the key drivers will help you budget effectively. Generally, you can expect to pay anywhere from €400 to €800 per square metre for a full renovation.

### Factors Influencing Renovation Costs

*   **Scope of Work**: Are you simply updating surfaces, or are you moving walls, reconfiguring plumbing, and replacing electrical systems? Extensive structural changes or layout modifications will increase costs.
*   **Material Quality**: The choice of materials significantly impacts the budget. For example, opting for premium porcelain tiles in Málaga, custom kitchens, or high-end taps and fittings will be more expensive than standard options. Dekorama offers a wide range of premium materials to suit various budgets and styles.
*   **Size of the Apartment**: Larger apartments naturally require more materials and labour, leading to higher overall costs.
*   **Condition of the Property**: Older properties may require more extensive foundational work, such as damp proofing, rewiring, or new plumbing, which can add to the expense.
*   **Access and Logistics**: Apartments on higher floors without lift access, or those in difficult-to-reach locations, might incur higher labour costs due to material transport challenges.
*   **Professional Fees**: Architects, interior designers, and project management fees are typically included in a comprehensive renovation quote.

### Budgeting Tips

When planning your budget, always allocate an additional 10-15% for unforeseen expenses. This contingency fund is crucial for dealing with unexpected issues that often arise in older properties. Discuss all potential costs upfront with your chosen renovation company to ensure transparency.

## The Renovation Process: Steps and Timeline

A full apartment renovation is a multi-stage process. Understanding each step helps manage expectations and ensures a smoother project. A typical timeline for a comprehensive renovation in Mijas is 3 to 6 months.

1.  **Initial Consultation and Design (2-4 weeks)**: This phase involves meeting with your renovation company, discussing your vision, needs, and budget. An architect or designer will create plans and 3D renderings. This is where you select materials, finishes, and fixtures.
2.  **Permit Application (4-8 weeks)**: Once designs are finalised, the necessary building permits (licencia de obra mayor) must be obtained from the Mijas Town Hall. This process can take several weeks, so it is vital to start early.
3.  **Demolition and Structural Work (2-4 weeks)**: Old fixtures are removed, walls are reconfigured, and any necessary structural reinforcements are made.
4.  **First Fixes (3-6 weeks)**: This includes installing new electrical wiring, plumbing pipes, and air conditioning ducts before walls are sealed.
5.  **Plastering and Flooring (2-4 weeks)**: Walls and ceilings are prepared, and new flooring is laid. This is often when you see the biggest visual transformation.
6.  **Second Fixes (3-5 weeks)**: Installation of custom kitchens, complete bathroom renovations, doors, windows, and built-in wardrobes. Tiling work is also completed here.
7.  **Painting and Finishing (2-3 weeks)**: The final touches, including painting, lighting installation, and fitting of switches and sockets.
8.  **Final Inspection and Handover (1 week)**: A thorough check of all work, addressing any snags, and final cleaning before you take possession of your newly renovated apartment.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Mijas

Obtaining the correct permits is a critical step for any significant renovation in Spain. For a 'reforma integral', you will almost certainly need a 'licencia de obra mayor' (major works permit) from the Mijas Town Hall. This permit requires detailed architectural plans and technical reports to ensure compliance with local building codes and safety standards.

Working with a reputable local renovation company like Dekorama is highly beneficial. They will have experience with the Mijas planning department and can manage the entire permit application process on your behalf, saving you time and potential headaches. Ignoring permit requirements can lead to fines, work stoppages, and even the demolition of unauthorised works. For general information on building regulations in Spain, you can consult official government resources, such as those provided by the Ministry of Transport, Mobility and Urban Agenda (MITMA) regarding housing and construction standards.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an architect for an apartment renovation in Mijas?
For a 'reforma integral' involving structural changes, layout modifications, or significant alterations to services, an architect or technical architect (aparejador) is legally required. They will draw up plans, secure permits, and supervise the work to ensure compliance with regulations.

### How long does it take to get a renovation permit in Mijas?
The time to obtain a major works permit ('licencia de obra mayor') from the Mijas Town Hall can vary, but typically ranges from 4 to 8 weeks. It is crucial to submit complete and accurate documentation to avoid delays in the process.

### Can I live in my apartment during a full renovation?
Living in your apartment during a full renovation is generally not advisable, especially during the demolition and 'first fix' stages. There will be significant dust, noise, and disruption, and essential services like water and electricity may be temporarily disconnected for safety and efficiency.

### What is the return on investment for an apartment renovation in Mijas?
A well-executed apartment renovation in Mijas can significantly increase your property's value and rental appeal. Modernised properties with updated kitchens, bathrooms, and energy-efficient systems often command higher prices and attract more tenants, offering a strong return on investment.
