## What Does a Full Home Renovation Involve?

A full home renovation is a comprehensive transformation of your property. It goes beyond cosmetic updates, often involving structural changes, new layouts, and complete overhauls of key areas. Understanding the typical stages helps you prepare for the journey.

### Initial Consultation and Design

The process begins with an in-depth consultation to understand your vision, needs, and budget. This phase involves site visits, architectural drawings, and interior design proposals. Whether you are dreaming of a sleek, modern aesthetic or a traditional Spanish villa feel, the design team will translate your ideas into a practical plan, including material selections and finishes.

### Permitting and Legalities

Before any physical work can commence, the renovation company will handle all necessary permits and legal documentation with the local council (Ayuntamiento). This typically includes applying for a *licencia de obra mayor* for significant structural changes or a *licencia de obra menor* for smaller, non-structural works. This stage can take several weeks or even months, so it is crucial to factor this into your overall timeline. You can find general information on local building regulations on your specific town hall's website, for example, the Ayuntamiento de Benalmádena.

### Demolition and Structural Work

Once permits are secured, the physical transformation begins. This involves demolition of existing structures, walls, or fixtures that are being removed. If your project includes reconfiguring layouts or adding extensions, structural work will be carried out, ensuring the integrity and safety of the building.

### Installations and Finishes

This is where your new home starts to take shape. New plumbing and electrical systems are installed, walls are built, and plastering is completed. This stage also includes fitting new windows and doors, installing flooring, and preparing surfaces for painting. Key areas like [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida) and [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos) are meticulously installed, using [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) chosen during the design phase.

### Final Touches and Handover

The final stage involves painting, installing light fixtures, fitting built-in wardrobes, and any other decorative elements. The site is thoroughly cleaned, and a final inspection is conducted to ensure everything meets the agreed-upon standards and your expectations. The project is then officially handed over to you, ready for you to enjoy your newly renovated home.

### Key Cost Factors

*   **Property Size and Condition:** Larger properties naturally incur higher costs. The existing condition of the property also plays a role; older homes requiring extensive structural repairs or system overhauls will be more expensive.
*   **Material Quality:** Your choice of materials has a major impact. Opting for high-end finishes, bespoke joinery, or designer fixtures will increase the overall price compared to standard options. Dekorama offers a wide range of premium materials to suit various budgets and styles.
*   **Scope and Complexity:** A simple cosmetic update is far less costly than a complete reconfiguration of the layout, including moving walls, relocating kitchens or bathrooms, or adding extensions.
*   **Permits and Fees:** Local council permits and technical architect fees are mandatory and add to the overall cost. These vary by municipality and project scope.

### Average Price Ranges

While it is challenging to give an exact figure without a detailed project brief, a general guideline for a full home renovation on the Costa del Sol is typically between €800 and €1,500+ per square metre. For a standard 100 square metre apartment, this could mean a budget of €80,000 to €150,000 or more. Luxury renovations with top-tier materials and complex designs can easily exceed these figures.

### Budgeting Tips

Always allocate an additional 10-15% of your total renovation budget for contingencies. Unexpected issues can arise, especially in older properties. A reputable company will provide a transparent breakdown of costs, helping you manage your finances effectively.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a full home renovation in Spain?
Yes, for most full home renovations involving structural changes, layout modifications, or significant alterations, you will need a *licencia de obra mayor* from your local town hall. Smaller, non-structural works may require a *licencia de obra menor*. Your renovation company can manage this process for you.

### How do I pay for a renovation project in Spain?
Typically, payment is structured in stages. An initial deposit is paid upon signing the contract, followed by instalments tied to the completion of specific project milestones. The final payment is usually made upon satisfactory completion and handover of the project.

### Can I renovate my property if I am not living in Spain?
Absolutely. Many expats and international owners renovate their properties remotely. Choosing a full-service renovation company like Dekorama, which offers comprehensive project management and regular communication, is ideal for ensuring your project progresses smoothly even from a distance.

### What kind of guarantee should I expect?
A reputable renovation company should provide guarantees on their workmanship, typically for a period of one to three years, and sometimes longer for structural elements. Manufacturers' warranties will also apply to the materials and appliances installed. Always clarify these terms in your contract.
