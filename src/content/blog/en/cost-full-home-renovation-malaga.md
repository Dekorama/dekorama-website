## What Factors Influence Renovation Costs in Málaga?
Several key elements directly impact the overall cost of your full home renovation. Understanding these will help you tailor your budget and expectations.

### Scope of Work
The extent of your renovation is the primary cost driver. A cosmetic update involving new paint and flooring will be significantly less expensive than a complete structural overhaul. Projects that include reconfiguring layouts, adding extensions, or relocating plumbing and electrical systems will naturally incur higher costs due to increased labour and material requirements. Major changes to kitchens and bathrooms are often the most significant investments within a full renovation.

### Material Quality
Your choice of materials plays a substantial role in the final price. Opting for standard, durable finishes will keep costs lower, while selecting premium materials, such as high-end [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos), bespoke cabinetry for [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida), or designer fittings for [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos), will increase your budget. Dekorama offers a wide range of [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) to suit various styles and budgets, ensuring quality regardless of your selection.

### Property Age and Condition
Older properties, common in many parts of the Costa del Sol, often come with hidden costs. They may require extensive updates to plumbing, electrical wiring, and insulation to meet modern standards and safety regulations. Structural repairs, damp proofing, or roof work can also add unexpected expenses. Newer properties generally require less foundational work, focusing more on aesthetic and layout changes.

### Permits and Fees
In Spain, all renovations require municipal permits. Minor works (`licencia de obra menor`) involve non-structural changes, while major works (`licencia de obra mayor`) are needed for structural alterations or extensions. The cost of these permits varies by municipality and the scale of the project. Engaging an architect or technical surveyor is often mandatory for major works, adding to professional fees. Always consult your local Ayuntamiento (town hall) for specific requirements and fees.

### Professional Fees
Beyond the construction work itself, you will need to account for professional services. These include architects, interior designers, project managers, and technical surveyors. Their expertise ensures compliance with regulations, optimal design, and efficient project execution. ## How Much Does a Full Home Renovation Cost in Málaga?
To provide a clearer picture, here is a general cost breakdown per square metre for different levels of full home renovations on the Costa del Sol. These figures are indicative and can fluctuate based on specific choices and market conditions.

*   **Basic Renovation (€600-€800 per sq m)**: This typically involves cosmetic updates such as new paint, flooring, updated light fixtures, and minor adjustments to kitchens and bathrooms using standard quality materials. It is suitable for refreshing a property without major structural changes.

*   **Mid-Range Renovation (€800-€1,000 per sq m)**: This level includes more significant updates, such as a complete overhaul of kitchens and bathrooms, new electrical and plumbing systems, new windows, and higher quality finishes. It aims for a modern, comfortable living space.

*   **Luxury Renovation (€1,000-€1,200+ per sq m)**: This category encompasses high-end materials, custom designs, smart home technology integration, and potentially structural modifications or extensions. It often involves bespoke solutions and premium finishes throughout the property.

For example, a 100-square-metre apartment undergoing a mid-range renovation could cost between €80,000 and €100,000. This estimate does not include furniture or appliances unless specified in the renovation contract.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an architect for my renovation in Málaga?
For major structural changes, extensions, or significant modifications to your property's layout, an architect or technical surveyor is legally required in Spain. For minor cosmetic updates, an architect may not be mandatory but can still offer valuable design and planning expertise.

### Can I live in my property during a full renovation?
Living in your property during a full renovation is generally not recommended due to noise, dust, lack of essential services like water or electricity, and safety concerns. It is usually more practical and safer to arrange alternative accommodation for the duration of the project.

### How can I save money on my renovation?
You can save money by prioritising essential upgrades, choosing mid-range materials where appropriate, doing some non-specialised tasks yourself (like painting), and getting multiple quotes. However, avoid cutting corners on structural work or essential services.

### What is the difference between `obra menor` and `obra mayor` permits?
`Obra menor` (minor works) permits are for non-structural changes like painting, flooring, or minor bathroom/kitchen updates. `Obra mayor` (major works) permits are for structural alterations, extensions, changes to the property's footprint, or significant utility overhauls, requiring an architect's plan.

### How long does it take to get a renovation permit in Málaga?
Processing times for renovation permits in Málaga vary. `Obra menor` permits can take a few weeks to a couple of months. `Obra mayor` permits, which require more detailed documentation and review, can take several months, sometimes up to six months or longer, depending on the complexity and local council workload.
