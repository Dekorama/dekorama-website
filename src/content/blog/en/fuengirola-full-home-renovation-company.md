## How Much Does a Full Home Renovation Cost in Fuengirola?
The cost of a full home renovation in Fuengirola varies significantly based on several factors. A basic renovation might start from €400 per square metre, while a high-end, luxury renovation with premium finishes and extensive structural changes could exceed €1,000 per square metre.

### Factors Affecting Renovation Costs
*   **Scope of Work**: Minor cosmetic updates are cheaper than structural changes, extensions, or complete overhauls of [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida) and [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos).
*   **Material Quality**: Choosing standard materials versus high-end, imported, or custom-made finishes will impact the budget considerably.
*   **Property Age and Condition**: Older properties often require more extensive work, such as rewiring, replumbing, or damp proofing, which adds to the cost.
*   **Permits and Fees**: Local council fees for permits and technical project plans are a necessary expense.
*   **Professional Fees**: Architects, interior designers, and project managers contribute to the overall cost.

### Budgeting Tips
Always allocate an contingency fund, typically 10-15% of your total budget, for unforeseen issues. Get detailed, itemised quotes from several companies. Be clear about your priorities and communicate your budget constraints upfront. A good renovation company will help you make informed decisions to stay within your financial plan.

## How Long Does a Full Home Renovation Take in Fuengirola?
The timeline for a full home renovation in Fuengirola depends on the project's complexity and the size of your property. Simple renovations might take 2-3 months, while extensive projects can last 6 months or more.

### Typical Project Phases
1.  **Planning and Design (2-4 weeks)**: Initial consultations, architectural plans, material selection, and permit applications.
2.  **Permit Approval (4-8 weeks)**: Waiting period for local council approval, which can vary.
3.  **Demolition (1-2 weeks)**: Removal of old structures, fixtures, and finishes.
4.  **Structural Work (2-4 weeks)**: Any necessary structural changes, new walls, or openings.
5.  **Installations (4-8 weeks)**: Plumbing, electrical, heating, and air conditioning systems.
6.  **Finishes (4-12 weeks)**: Flooring, tiling, plastering, painting, kitchen and bathroom installations, carpentry, and final touches.

## Frequently asked questions
### Do I need an architect for a full renovation in Fuengirola?
For significant structural changes, extensions, or alterations affecting the building's exterior, an architect's plan and supervision are legally required in Spain. For minor cosmetic renovations, an architect may not be necessary, but a qualified technical surveyor (aparejador) is often needed for permits.

### How do I ensure my renovation company is reliable?
Check their local registration, ask for references from previous clients, and review their portfolio of completed projects. Ensure they have comprehensive insurance and provide a clear, detailed contract outlining all aspects of the work, timelines, and payment schedules. Transparent communication is key.

### Can I live in my property during a full renovation?
It is generally not advisable to live in your property during a full renovation, especially if it involves extensive structural work, plumbing, or electrical changes. The disruption, dust, noise, and lack of essential services can make it uncomfortable and unsafe. Plan for alternative accommodation.

### What permits are required for renovations in Fuengirola?
Minor works (obra menor) like painting or tiling usually require a simple communication or a minor permit. Major works (obra mayor) involving structural changes, extensions, or significant layout alterations require a full building permit, an architect's project, and a technical supervisor.
