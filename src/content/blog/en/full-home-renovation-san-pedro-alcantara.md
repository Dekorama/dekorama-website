## How much does a full home renovation cost in San Pedro de Alcantara?
The cost of a full home renovation in San Pedro de Alcantara can vary widely, influenced by several key factors. Understanding these elements will help you set a realistic budget for your project.

### Key Cost Factors:
*   **Property Size:** Larger properties naturally require more materials and labour, increasing the overall cost.
*   **Scope of Work:** A complete overhaul involving structural changes, new plumbing, and electrical systems will be more expensive than a purely cosmetic update.
*   **Material Quality:** The choice of materials significantly impacts the budget. Opting for premium materials like high-end porcelain tiles, custom kitchens, and designer taps will elevate costs compared to standard options. Dekorama offers a wide range of premium materials to suit various budgets and styles.
*   **Labour Costs:** Skilled tradespeople and experienced project managers command higher rates, but their expertise often ensures a smoother process and a superior finish.
*   **Permits and Fees:** Local council permits (licencias de obra) and technical reports from architects or engineers are mandatory for most significant renovations in Spain. These fees must be factored into your budget.
*   **Unexpected Issues:** Older properties, common in areas like San Pedro de Alcantara, may reveal unforeseen issues such as dampness, outdated wiring, or structural problems during demolition. It is wise to allocate a contingency fund, typically 10-15% of the total budget, for these eventualities.

## How long does a full home renovation take in San Pedro de Alcantara?
The timeline for a full home renovation in San Pedro de Alcantara is as variable as the cost, depending on the project's complexity and efficiency. A well-managed project with clear communication can significantly reduce delays.

### Typical Renovation Phases and Timelines:
1.  **Planning and Design (4-8 weeks):** This initial phase involves consultations, architectural drawings, material selection, and detailed quotation. This is where your vision takes shape with the help of design professionals.
2.  **Permit Acquisition (4-12 weeks):** Obtaining the necessary building permits (licencias de obra) from the local council, such as the Ayuntamiento de Marbella for San Pedro de Alcantara, can be a lengthy process. The duration depends on the complexity of the project and the council's workload. It is crucial to start this early.
3.  **Demolition and Structural Work (2-4 weeks):** Once permits are secured, old fixtures are removed, and any necessary structural modifications are made. This phase can be noisy and disruptive.
4.  **Installations (4-8 weeks):** New plumbing, electrical wiring, heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems are installed. This is a critical stage that lays the groundwork for the finishes.
5.  **Finishing Works (6-12 weeks):** This includes plastering, painting, flooring installation (e.g., porcelain tiles in Málaga), fitting custom kitchens, and installing complete bathroom renovations, doors, and built-in wardrobes. Attention to detail here is paramount for the final aesthetic.
6.  **Final Touches and Handover (1-2 weeks):** Cleaning, final inspections, and addressing any minor snags before the property is officially handed over.

For an average 100-square-metre apartment, a full renovation typically spans 3 to 6 months. Larger or more complex projects can extend beyond this timeframe.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations for Expats
Understanding Spanish building regulations is vital for any renovation project on the Costa del Sol. For expats, this can seem daunting, but working with an experienced local company simplifies the process.

In San Pedro de Alcantara, as part of Marbella, you will need to apply for a *Licencia de Obra* (building permit) from the Marbella Town Hall. There are two main types:

*   **Licencia de Obra Menor (Minor Works Permit):** Required for cosmetic changes that do not affect the structure or layout, such as painting, changing flooring, or updating bathrooms without altering plumbing locations. These are generally quicker and less expensive to obtain.
*   **Licencia de Obra Mayor (Major Works Permit):** Essential for structural modifications, changes to the property's layout, extensions, or alterations to plumbing and electrical systems. This requires detailed architectural plans and typically involves longer processing times and higher fees.

## Frequently asked questions
### Do I need an architect for a full renovation in San Pedro de Alcantara?
Yes, for any full renovation involving structural changes, layout modifications, or significant alterations to plumbing and electrical systems, an architect's plans and supervision are legally required to obtain a *Licencia de Obra Mayor* from the local council.

### Can I live in my property during the renovation?
It is generally not recommended to live in your property during a full renovation due to noise, dust, and safety concerns. For extensive projects, temporary relocation is usually necessary to ensure comfort and allow work to proceed efficiently.

### What are the typical payment terms for a renovation project?
Payment terms typically involve an initial deposit (10-30%) to start the project, followed by staged payments linked to project milestones (e.g., completion of demolition, structural work, installations, finishes). A final payment is made upon satisfactory completion.

### How do I choose a reliable renovation contractor on the Costa del Sol?
Look for contractors with extensive experience, a strong portfolio of completed projects, positive client testimonials, and proper licensing and insurance. A company that offers a full-service approach, like Dekorama, can simplify the process for expats.

### What kind of warranty or guarantee should I expect?
Reputable renovation companies typically offer guarantees on their workmanship and materials. In Spain, there is a legal ten-year structural guarantee for new builds and major structural renovations.
