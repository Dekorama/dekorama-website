## What Does a Full Villa Renovation in Manilva Involve?
A full villa renovation, or "reforma integral", transforms your property from top to bottom, addressing structural elements, aesthetics, and functionality. It goes beyond cosmetic updates, aiming to modernise and enhance your home's value and living experience. For villas in Manilva, this often means adapting older properties to contemporary standards while respecting their unique charm.

### Structural Changes and Layout Redesign
This can include moving or removing internal walls to create open-plan living spaces, adding extensions, or reconfiguring rooms for better flow. Structural work requires careful planning and adherence to local building codes, ensuring the integrity and safety of your villa. It is a fundamental step in modernising older properties.

### Kitchen and Bathroom Transformations
These areas are often the focal points of any renovation. A complete overhaul involves replacing all fixtures, fittings, tiles, and cabinetry. For a custom kitchen, this means designing a space that is both beautiful and highly functional, incorporating modern appliances and storage solutions. Similarly, complete bathroom renovations focus on creating luxurious, efficient, and relaxing environments.

### Electrical and Plumbing Upgrades
Many older villas in Manilva benefit significantly from updated electrical and plumbing systems. This ensures compliance with current safety standards, improves energy efficiency, and prevents future issues. Rewiring, installing new fuse boxes, upgrading pipework, and adding modern heating or cooling systems are common components of this phase.

### Exterior and Landscaping Considerations
A full villa renovation often extends beyond the interior. This might include façade restoration, roof repairs, upgrading windows and doors for better insulation, or redesigning outdoor living areas. Enhancing terraces, pools, and gardens adds significant value and enjoyment to your Manilva property, making the most of the Costa del Sol lifestyle.

## How Much Does a Full Villa Renovation Cost in Manilva?
Understanding the potential costs is paramount when planning a full villa renovation in Manilva. Prices can vary significantly based on several factors, but having a clear estimate helps in setting a realistic budget. Generally, you should anticipate costs ranging from moderate to high, reflecting the quality of materials and the complexity of the work.

### Factors Influencing Renovation Costs
The final price tag for your villa renovation is shaped by several key elements:
*   **Scope of Work**: A complete overhaul, including structural changes, will naturally cost more than a renovation focused purely on interiors.
*   **Material Quality**: Choosing premium materials like high-end porcelain tiles or bespoke cabinetry will increase costs compared to standard options. Dekorama offers a wide range of premium materials, including porcelain tiles in Málaga.
*   **Finishes and Fixtures**: The level of luxury in finishes, from flooring to lighting, plays a significant role.
*   **Property Size and Age**: Larger villas require more materials and labour. Older properties often need more extensive structural or system upgrades.
*   **Accessibility**: Difficult access to the property can increase labour and transport costs.
*   **Professional Fees**: Architects, engineers, and project managers add to the overall budget.

### Cost Breakdown: Per Square Metre Estimates
As a general guide for a full villa renovation in Manilva:
*   **Basic Renovation**: €800 - €1,000 per square metre. This includes good quality standard materials, updated systems, and minor layout changes.
*   **Mid-Range Renovation**: €1,000 - €1,250 per square metre. This allows for higher quality materials, more significant layout modifications, and modern finishes in kitchens and bathrooms.
*   **High-End/Luxury Renovation**: €1,250 - €1,500+ per square metre. This category involves premium materials, custom designs, advanced home automation, and extensive structural work.
These figures are estimates and should be confirmed with detailed quotes from reputable renovation companies.

### Budgeting for Unexpected Expenses
It is always wise to allocate an additional 10-15% of your total renovation budget for unforeseen issues. During renovations, especially in older properties, unexpected problems like hidden damp, outdated wiring, or structural surprises can emerge. Having a contingency fund prevents delays and financial stress, ensuring your project stays on track.

## How Long Does a Villa Renovation Take in Manilva?
The timeline for a full villa renovation in Manilva can vary widely, influenced by the project's complexity, the size of the property, and the efficiency of the renovation team. While some smaller projects might conclude in a few months, a comprehensive villa overhaul typically requires a longer commitment.

### Project Phases and Timelines
A typical full villa renovation can be broken down into several key phases:
*   **Planning and Design (1-3 months)**: This initial stage involves consultations, architectural drawings, material selection, and obtaining necessary permits. This phase is crucial for defining the scope and avoiding costly changes later.
*   **Demolition and Structural Work (2-6 weeks)**: Once permits are secured, existing structures are removed, and any new walls or extensions are built.
*   **First Fix (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC) (4-8 weeks)**: All hidden systems are installed or upgraded before walls are closed up.
*   **Plastering, Flooring, and Tiling (4-8 weeks)**: Surfaces are prepared, and premium materials are laid.
*   **Second Fix (Kitchens, Bathrooms, Doors, Fittings) (4-8 weeks)**: Custom kitchens and complete bathroom renovations are installed, along with internal doors, lighting, and other fixtures.
*   **Finishing Touches and Landscaping (2-4 weeks)**: Painting, final cleaning, and any exterior landscaping work are completed.
Overall, a full villa renovation in Manilva can range from 4 months for a smaller, straightforward project to 12 months or more for a large, complex villa with significant structural changes.

### Impact of Permits and Approvals
Obtaining the necessary building permits from the Manilva Town Hall can significantly impact your project timeline. The processing time for a "Licencia de Obra Mayor" (Major Works Permit) can range from a few weeks to several months, depending on the municipality's workload and the completeness of your application. Starting this process early is essential to prevent delays.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations for Expats
Understanding Spanish building regulations and permit requirements is critical for any renovation project on the Costa del Sol, especially for expats. Failing to secure the correct permits can lead to fines, project stoppages, and even the demolition of unauthorised work.

### Licencia de Obra Mayor (Major Works Permit)
For a full villa renovation that involves structural changes, significant layout modifications, or alterations to the exterior façade, you will need a "Licencia de Obra Mayor." This major works permit requires a detailed technical project drafted by a qualified architect or technical engineer. It ensures your renovation complies with local planning laws, safety standards, and environmental regulations.
You can find more information on local planning regulations through the Manilva Town Hall website or the Junta de Andalucía's urban planning department.

### Technical Project and Architect's Role
An architect plays a pivotal role in a major renovation. They will:
*   Draft the comprehensive technical project, including plans, specifications, and calculations.
*   Ensure compliance with all relevant building codes and municipal ordinances.
*   Manage the permit application process on your behalf.
*   Supervise the construction work to ensure it adheres to the approved plans.
Choosing an architect with experience in Manilva and a good understanding of expat needs can streamline the entire process.
