## What Defines Modern Bathroom Design?
Modern bathroom design is characterised by a commitment to simplicity, functionality, and uncluttered spaces. It moves away from ornate details, favouring sleek surfaces and a harmonious blend of natural and engineered materials. The goal is to create a tranquil, spa-like environment that is both beautiful and highly practical.

Key characteristics include:
*   **Minimalist Aesthetic**: Clean lines, geometric shapes, and an absence of unnecessary ornamentation.
*   **Neutral Colour Palettes**: Often featuring shades of white, grey, beige, and black, with accents of natural wood or stone.
*   **Openness and Light**: Maximising natural light and using clever lighting schemes to enhance space.
*   **Functionality**: Every element serves a purpose, with an emphasis on ergonomic design and efficient storage.
*   **Integration of Technology**: Smart mirrors, digital showers, and underfloor heating are common additions.

Modern fixtures combine sleek design with advanced functionality.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a bathroom renovation typically take?
A standard modern bathroom renovation usually takes between 3 to 6 weeks from the start of demolition to the final touches. The exact timeline depends on the bathroom's size, the complexity of the design, and the availability of materials and tradespeople.

### Do I need a permit for a bathroom renovation in Torremolinos?
Yes, for significant structural changes, moving plumbing, or altering the layout, you will likely need a *licencia de obra* (building permit) from the Torremolinos Town Hall. Cosmetic updates like painting or replacing tiles might not require one, but it is always best to check with local authorities or your renovation company.
