## What Are the Key Regulations for Holiday Rentals in Andalusia?

Operating a holiday rental in Andalusia, including Nerja, requires adherence to specific regional regulations. These rules ensure guest safety, quality standards, and fair competition. Ignoring them can lead to fines and legal issues, so it is vital to be fully compliant.

### Tourist Licence Requirements

To legally operate a holiday rental, your property must be registered with the *Registro de Turismo de Andalucía* (Andalusian Tourism Register). This involves obtaining a *licencia de primera ocupación* (first occupation licence) or an equivalent document, proving the property meets habitability standards. You must also submit a *declaración responsable* (responsible declaration) to the Junta de Andalucía, stating that your property complies with all requirements. These requirements include having air conditioning in bedrooms and living rooms, first aid kits, tourist information, and complaint forms. For detailed information, consult the official Junta de Andalucía tourism website.

### Technical Building Code (CTE)

Any significant renovation work in Spain must comply with the *Código Técnico de la Edificación* (Technical Building Code or CTE). This national regulation covers aspects such as structural safety, fire safety, energy efficiency, and accessibility. For minor cosmetic changes, the impact might be minimal. However, for more extensive projects, such as reconfiguring layouts or major electrical/plumbing overhauls, you will need to engage a qualified architect or technical surveyor to ensure compliance and obtain necessary municipal permits. This is particularly important for ensuring the long-term safety and legality of your investment.

### Budgeting for Key Areas

Here is a general breakdown of typical costs for a comprehensive renovation:

*   **Kitchen Renovation:** A modern, functional kitchen is a major draw for holidaymakers. Costs can range from €5,000 to €15,000, covering new units, appliances, worktops, and tiling. Opt for durable, easy-to-clean surfaces.
*   **Bathroom Renovation:** Guests expect clean, contemporary bathrooms. A complete bathroom renovation, including new sanitaryware, tiling, and shower screens, typically costs between €3,000 and €8,000 per bathroom. Consider a complete bathroom renovation for maximum impact.
*   **Flooring:** Replacing old flooring with modern, hard-wearing options like porcelain tiles in Málaga can transform a space. Expect to pay €20-€60 per square metre for materials and installation.
*   **Painting and Decorating:** A fresh, neutral colour palette is essential. Budget €1,500 to €4,000 for painting a typical two-bedroom apartment.
*   **Electrical and Plumbing Updates:** If your property is older, updating these systems is crucial for safety and functionality. This can add €3,000 to €10,000, depending on the extent of the work.
*   **Furniture and Furnishings:** Beyond the structural renovation, furnishing your apartment for comfort and style is vital. Budget €5,000 to €15,000 for quality, durable furniture, bedding, and decor.

These figures are estimates. Engaging a reputable renovation company for a detailed quote is always recommended. For larger projects, such as full home renovations on the Costa del Sol, a tailored budget is essential.

Yes, for any structural changes, layout modifications, or significant alterations to plumbing or electrical systems, you will need a municipal building permit (licencia de obra).
