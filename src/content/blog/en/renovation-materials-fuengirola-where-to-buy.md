### Budget checklist before you compare quotes

- Ask for a site measurement instead of relying on a rough message-based estimate.
- Check whether waste removal, permits and communal-area protection are included.
- Review exact specifications for tiles, fittings, shower trays, worktops or cabinetry.
- Confirm realistic supply times so the installation phase does not stall halfway through.

Once those points are clear, comparing two proposals becomes far more reliable. It also becomes easier to spot a cheap-looking quote that is only cheaper because several awkward but necessary items have been left out.

### What usually pushes the cost or timeline up

Budgets tend to grow when old plumbing or electrics need replacing, walls or floors require levelling, layouts change or key materials have long lead times. Quotes are much easier to compare when labour, materials and contingency items are separated clearly instead of bundled into vague allowances.

In older Costa del Sol flats, the real condition of drains, partitions, existing flooring and joinery can also change the final scope. That is why an on-site technical review is far more useful than a fixed number produced without seeing the property properly.

### How to reduce friction before work starts

It helps to lock in finishes, final measurements and service points before the team starts on site. Access restrictions, community rules and property protection should also be agreed in advance. That preparation usually saves rework, avoids rushed decisions and keeps the schedule more predictable.

If orders, delivery dates and the sequence of trades are coordinated before the start date, the project usually runs much more smoothly. It does not remove every risk, but it does cut down avoidable stoppages and last-minute changes.
