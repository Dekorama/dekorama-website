A full home renovation in Estepona generally ranges from €800 to €1,500 per square metre. For an average 80-100m² property, this means a total cost between €64,000 and €150,000. Projects typically take 3 to 6 months, though larger or more complex renovations can extend beyond this timeframe.

## How Much Does a Full Home Renovation Cost in Estepona?
The cost of a full home renovation in Estepona is highly variable, influenced by several key factors. While a general per-square-metre estimate provides a starting point, a detailed breakdown helps in understanding where your money goes. Expect costs to be higher for older properties requiring extensive structural work or complete overhauls of plumbing and electrical systems.

Here is a general cost guide for different renovation levels:

### Basic Renovation (around €800-€1,000 per m²)
This level includes essential updates such as painting, minor flooring changes, basic kitchen and bathroom refurbishments, and updating fixtures. It focuses on improving aesthetics and functionality without major structural alterations. Materials are typically standard quality.

### Mid-Range Renovation (around €1,000-€1,250 per m²)
This involves more comprehensive changes, including new flooring throughout, a complete overhaul of kitchens and bathrooms with mid-range materials, updated electrical and plumbing systems, and some reconfiguring of internal layouts. This level offers a significant upgrade in comfort and style.

### High-End Renovation (around €1,250-€1,500+ per m²)
For those seeking luxury and bespoke finishes, high-end renovations involve premium materials, custom-designed kitchens and bathrooms, smart home technology integration, underfloor heating, and potentially structural modifications. This category allows for unique design elements and top-tier craftsmanship.

## Key Factors Influencing Renovation Costs
Understanding the elements that drive renovation costs is essential for effective budgeting. Each aspect contributes to the overall expense, and choices made in one area can significantly impact the total.

### Scope of Work
This is the most significant factor. A complete gut renovation, involving structural changes, re-piping, and re-wiring, will naturally cost more than a cosmetic update. The extent of demolition and new construction directly impacts labour and material costs.

### Material Quality
From [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos) to kitchen countertops and bathroom fittings, the quality of materials chosen plays a huge role. Opting for [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) like natural stone, high-end cabinetry, or designer taps will increase costs, but also enhance durability and aesthetic appeal.

### Labour Costs
Skilled tradespeople are crucial for a quality renovation. Labour rates vary, but experienced professionals ensure high standards of work. The complexity of the design and the required expertise will influence labour hours and associated costs.

### Kitchen and Bathroom Renovations
These rooms are typically the most expensive per square metre due to the concentration of plumbing, electrical work, tiling, and specialised fittings. A [custom kitchen](/en/cocinas-a-medida) or [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos) can account for a substantial portion of the overall budget.

### Permits and Fees
Local regulations in Estepona require permits for most significant renovation work. These fees vary based on the project's scope and local council charges. Always factor these administrative costs into your budget.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Estepona
Undertaking a renovation in Estepona requires adherence to local planning laws and obtaining the correct permits. Failing to do so can lead to fines, project delays, or even the demolition of unauthorised work. It is vital to work with a professional who understands Spanish building regulations.

Most significant renovation works will require a *Licencia de Obra Mayor* (Major Works Permit) or *Licencia de Obra Menor* (Minor Works Permit) from the Estepona Town Hall. The type of permit depends on the scope of work. Structural changes, extensions, or alterations to the building's facade typically require a Major Works Permit, which involves submitting detailed architectural plans.

It is highly recommended to engage an architect or a qualified renovation company that can manage the permit application process on your behalf. They will ensure all plans comply with local urban planning regulations. You can find general information on Spanish building regulations through official government channels, such as the Ministry of Public Works (Ministerio de Fomento) or your local Town Hall website.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a full renovation in Estepona?
Yes, most full home renovations in Estepona require a building permit (Licencia de Obra) from the local Town Hall. The specific type of permit depends on the scope of work, especially if structural changes are involved. It is crucial to obtain the correct permits to avoid fines or legal issues.

### How can I save money on my renovation in Estepona?
You can save money by carefully planning, prioritising essential upgrades, and choosing mid-range materials where appropriate. Consider keeping the existing layout if possible to reduce plumbing and electrical costs. Getting multiple detailed quotes also helps ensure competitive pricing.

### What is the average cost of a kitchen renovation in Estepona?
A kitchen renovation in Estepona can range from €8,000 for a basic update to over €30,000 for a high-end, custom-designed kitchen. Factors like material quality, appliance choices, and the extent of structural changes significantly influence the final price.

### How long does it take to get renovation permits in Estepona?
The time to obtain renovation permits in Estepona varies. Minor works permits might take a few weeks, while major works permits, requiring more detailed plans and reviews, can take several months. It is advisable to start the permit application process well in advance of your desired renovation start date.

### Is it more expensive to renovate an old property in Estepona?
Renovating an older property in Estepona can often be more expensive. Older homes may require extensive updates to plumbing, electrical systems, and potentially structural reinforcements or damp proofing. These hidden issues can add significant costs and extend the project timeline compared to renovating a newer property.
