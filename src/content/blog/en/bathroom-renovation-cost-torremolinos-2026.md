## What Influences the Cost of a Bathroom Renovation in Torremolinos?
Several critical factors determine the final price of your bathroom renovation. Being aware of these elements from the outset will help you plan your budget more accurately and avoid unexpected expenses.

### Size and Layout
The dimensions of your bathroom play a direct role in the cost. Larger bathrooms naturally require more materials and labour. Furthermore, if you plan to alter the existing layout, such as moving the toilet, shower, or sink, this will involve significant plumbing adjustments. Relocating pipework and drainage is a complex task that adds considerably to both the cost and the project timeline. Minor cosmetic changes are far less expensive than a complete reconfiguration of the space.

### Material Choices
Your selection of materials is one of the biggest cost drivers. From tiles to sanitaryware, the market offers an enormous range of options. Opting for standard ceramic tiles and basic fixtures will keep costs down, while choosing `premium materials` like large format `porcelain tiles in Málaga`, natural stone, custom-made vanity units, or designer taps will elevate the budget. Dekorama offers a wide selection of high-quality materials to suit various styles and price points, ensuring durability and aesthetic appeal.

### Labour and Professional Services
The expertise of the renovation team is invaluable. Skilled plumbers, electricians, tilers, and general contractors ensure the work is done correctly, safely, and to a high standard. While it might be tempting to choose the cheapest quote, experienced professionals often save you money in the long run by preventing costly mistakes and ensuring longevity. A reputable company like Dekorama provides a comprehensive service, managing all trades and aspects of your project.

### Fixtures and Fittings
Every item in your bathroom, from the shower tray and screen to the taps, toilet, and basin, contributes to the overall cost. Standard white ceramic options are budget-friendly, but if you desire specific brands, smart features, walk-in showers, or bespoke shower screens, the price will increase. Consider the balance between functionality, aesthetics, and your budget when selecting these essential components.

### Unexpected Issues
Older properties, common across the Costa del Sol, can hide unforeseen problems. During demolition, issues like outdated plumbing, faulty electrical wiring, damp, or structural imperfections might be uncovered. Addressing these problems is crucial for the safety and longevity of your new bathroom, but they will add to the overall cost and potentially extend the timeline. A contingency fund, typically 10-15% of your budget, is highly recommended to cover such eventualities.

## The Renovation Process: What to Expect
Understanding the typical stages of a complete bathroom renovation helps you prepare and manage expectations. A well-organised process ensures efficiency and minimises disruption.

### Planning and Design
The first step involves detailed consultation and design. This is where you discuss your vision, needs, and budget with your chosen renovation company. Measurements are taken, material samples are reviewed, and a detailed plan, including 3D renderings, is often created. This stage ensures everyone is aligned on the project's scope and aesthetic goals.

### Permits and Regulations
Before any work begins, it is crucial to address local regulations. Most significant bathroom renovations in Torremolinos require a `licencia de obra menor` (minor works permit) from the local council, especially if structural changes, plumbing, or electrical work are involved. Your contractor should assist with this process. For specific requirements in Torremolinos, it is advisable to consult the official Torremolinos Town Hall website or a local architect to ensure compliance.

### Demolition and Preparation
Once permits are secured, the physical work commences with the demolition phase. Old fixtures, tiles, and any non-structural elements are removed. This stage also involves preparing the surfaces for new installations, which may include levelling floors or repairing walls. Proper waste disposal is managed by the renovation team, ensuring a clean and safe work environment.

### Installation of New Elements
This is the core construction phase. New plumbing and electrical systems are installed or updated. This is followed by waterproofing, tiling, and the installation of sanitaryware such as the toilet, basin, shower tray, and bath. Shower screens, vanity units, and mirrors are then fitted. Each step is carried out by skilled tradespeople to ensure quality and precision.

### Finishing Touches
The final stage involves painting, installing lighting fixtures, fitting accessories like towel rails and toilet roll holders, and a thorough clean-up. The renovation team will conduct a final inspection with you to ensure every detail meets your expectations and the agreed-upon design. This is when your vision truly comes to life.
