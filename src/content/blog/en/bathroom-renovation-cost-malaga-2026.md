## Key Factors Influencing Your Bathroom Renovation Budget
Understanding the components that drive renovation costs helps you make informed decisions and manage your budget effectively.

### Material Choices
The selection of materials is arguably the most significant cost driver. The difference between standard ceramic tiles and high-end `porcelain tiles in Málaga` can be substantial. Similarly, designer `taps & fittings` from European brands will cost more than basic options. Consider the quality of your `shower trays` and `shower screens`, as these also vary widely in price and durability. Opting for `premium materials` not only enhances aesthetics but also contributes to the longevity and value of your bathroom.

### Labour and Professional Fees
Labour costs account for a large portion of the renovation budget. This includes the wages for skilled professionals such as plumbers, electricians, tilers, plasterers, and general builders. The complexity of the work, the need for specialised trades, and the duration of the project all impact labour expenses. Engaging an experienced company like DEKORAMA ensures that all trades are coordinated efficiently, reducing potential delays and additional costs.

### Bathroom Size and Layout Changes
A larger bathroom naturally requires more materials and labour. However, the most significant cost increase often comes from altering the existing layout. Moving the toilet, shower, or sink requires extensive plumbing work, which is both time-consuming and expensive. If you are keeping the existing plumbing points, costs will be considerably lower. Any structural changes, such as moving walls, will also add to the complexity and cost.

### Permits and Regulations
In Spain, most renovation work requires a municipal permit, known as a *Licencia de Obra*. For a complete bathroom renovation that does not involve structural changes, a *Licencia de Obra Menor* (minor works permit) is typically sufficient. If you plan to alter the structure or significantly change the layout, a *Licencia de Obra Mayor* (major works permit) may be necessary. The cost of these permits varies by municipality (e.g., Benalmádena, Marbella, Fuengirola) and the scope of work. Always factor in these administrative fees and the time required for approval. You can find general information on building permits in Spain through official government portals or by consulting with your local town hall (Ayuntamiento).
