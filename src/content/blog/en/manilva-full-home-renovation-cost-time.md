## How Much Does a Full Home Renovation Cost in Manilva?
The cost of a full home renovation in Manilva varies significantly based on several factors. These include the size of your property, the quality of materials you choose, the extent of structural changes, and the complexity of installations like custom kitchens or complete bathroom renovations. A basic renovation might involve cosmetic updates, while a high-end project could include structural modifications, premium materials, and bespoke finishes.

### Key Cost Factors
*   **Materials**: This is a major variable. Choosing standard porcelain tiles in Málaga versus luxury imported stone, or basic taps versus designer fittings, will heavily impact your budget. Dekorama offers a wide range of premium materials to suit various styles and budgets.
*   **Labour**: Skilled tradespeople are essential for quality work. Labour costs account for a significant portion of the budget, covering demolition, construction, plumbing, electrical work, tiling, painting, and finishing.

## What Influences Renovation Timelines in Manilva?
Just like costs, renovation timelines are not fixed. A small apartment might take a few weeks, while a large villa requiring extensive structural changes could take over half a year. Understanding the factors that affect the schedule helps in setting realistic expectations.

### Factors Affecting Duration
*   **Scope of Work**: A full home renovation that includes reconfiguring layouts, moving walls, or adding extensions will naturally take longer than one focused purely on aesthetic upgrades.
*   **Permit Acquisition**: Obtaining the necessary building permits from the Manilva Ayuntamiento can take several weeks or even months. This administrative process is often the first hurdle and can delay the start of physical work.
*   **Material Availability**: Sourcing specific or imported materials can extend timelines, especially if there are supply chain delays. Planning material selection well in advance is crucial.
*   **Contractor Efficiency and Team Size**: An experienced renovation company with a well-organised team can work more efficiently. Dekorama's 20+ years of experience in full home renovations on the Costa del Sol ensures streamlined project management.
*   **Weather Conditions**: While Manilva enjoys excellent weather, heavy rain can occasionally impact outdoor work or material deliveries, particularly during winter months.
*   **Client Decisions**: Delays in making decisions on materials, finishes, or design changes can also push back the completion date.

Typically, a full home renovation in Manilva can take anywhere from 3 to 8 months. This includes the initial design phase, permit application, demolition, construction, installation of new elements, and final touches.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Manilva
Understanding the local regulations and obtaining the correct permits is a critical step for any renovation project in Manilva. Failing to do so can lead to fines, project delays, or even the forced demolition of unauthorised work.

For major structural changes, extensions, or significant alterations to the property's exterior, you will need a `Licencia de Obra Mayor` (Major Works Permit) from the Manilva Town Hall. For smaller, non-structural cosmetic changes like repainting or replacing tiles, a `Licencia de Obra Menor` (Minor Works Permit) is usually sufficient. Your chosen renovation company should guide you through this process.

It is advisable to consult with a local expert or refer to official guidelines regarding Spanish building regulations to ensure full compliance. Reputable renovation companies, like Dekorama, handle all necessary paperwork and liaise with the local authorities on your behalf, simplifying the process for expats.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an architect for a full renovation in Manilva?
For major structural changes, extensions, or significant alterations to your property's exterior, an architect is legally required to draw up plans and oversee the work. For smaller, cosmetic renovations, an architect may not be necessary, but a reputable builder will still manage the project professionally.

### How do I get a building permit in Manilva?
Your chosen renovation company should handle the permit application process for you. They will prepare the necessary documentation, submit it to the Manilva Town Hall (Ayuntamiento), and follow up on its approval. The type of permit depends on the scope of your renovation.

### Can I live in my property during a full renovation?
Living in your property during a full renovation is generally not advisable, especially for extensive projects involving dust, noise, and utility disruptions. It is often more comfortable and safer to arrange alternative accommodation for the duration of the major works.

### What is a typical payment schedule for a renovation in Spain?
Payment schedules usually involve an initial deposit (10-30%), followed by stage payments tied to project milestones (e.g., completion of demolition, structural work, tiling). A final payment is typically made upon satisfactory completion and handover of the project.
