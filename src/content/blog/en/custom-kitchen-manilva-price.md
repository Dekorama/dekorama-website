## How Much Does a Custom Kitchen in Manilva Cost?
The price of a custom kitchen in Manilva is not a fixed figure; it is a dynamic sum influenced by several key elements. To provide a clearer picture, we can categorise costs based on the scope and luxury level of the project. These figures include design, materials, and installation, but exclude major structural changes to your property.

*   **Budget-Friendly Custom Kitchens (€8,000 - €15,000):** These projects focus on maximising value without compromising on quality. Expect durable, mid-range materials for cabinetry (e.g., laminate, melamine), standard quartz or compact laminate countertops, and essential, energy-efficient appliances. The design will be practical and efficient, optimising space.

*   **Mid-Range Custom Kitchens (€15,000 - €25,000):** This category allows for greater customisation and higher-quality finishes. You might choose lacquered or wood veneer cabinets, premium quartz or natural stone countertops, and a broader selection of integrated appliances. This level includes premium solid wood or bespoke painted cabinetry, exotic natural stone or high-performance composite countertops, top-of-the-range smart appliances, and sophisticated lighting and smart home integrations. Complex layouts, custom joinery, and designer fixtures are standard at this price point.

## What Factors Influence Custom Kitchen Prices in Manilva?
Understanding the components that make up the total cost is essential for effective budgeting. Each choice you make, from materials to appliances, directly impacts the final price.

### Materials and Finishes
This is often the largest variable. Cabinetry, countertops, flooring, and wall tiles all come in a vast range of prices and qualities. For instance, porcelain tiles in Málaga offer durability and style, but their cost varies depending on size, finish, and origin. Solid wood cabinets are typically more expensive than laminate, and natural stone countertops like granite or marble will cost more than engineered quartz or compact laminates. Your choice of taps, sinks, and other fixtures also contributes significantly.

### Appliances
The appliances you select can dramatically alter the overall cost. High-end integrated appliances with smart features will naturally be more expensive than standard freestanding models. Consider energy efficiency ratings, brand reputation, and specific functionalities when making your choices. Often, a significant portion of the budget is allocated here, as appliances are central to kitchen functionality.

### Labour and Installation
Skilled labour is a premium, especially for custom work. The complexity of your design, the need for bespoke carpentry, and the intricacy of tile laying or countertop fabrication will all affect labour costs. A full-service renovation company like Dekorama manages all trades, ensuring a seamless and efficient installation process, which can save you time and potential headaches compared to coordinating multiple independent contractors.

### Design and Planning
Professional design services are invaluable for a custom kitchen. An experienced designer will optimise your space, advise on material choices, and ensure functionality and aesthetics align with your vision. While some companies include design fees in their overall quote, others may charge separately. Investing in good design prevents costly mistakes and ensures a result you will love.

### Permits and Regulations
In Spain, most significant renovation projects, including a complete kitchen overhaul, require a municipal building permit (licencia de obra). The type of permit (obra menor or obra mayor) depends on the scope of work. Obtaining these permits involves fees and can add to the project timeline. It is crucial to work with a company that understands local regulations in Manilva and the wider Costa del Sol to ensure compliance. You can find more information on local planning requirements on your local council website or the regional government planning department.

### Additional Features
Beyond the basics, many custom kitchens include features that enhance convenience and luxury. These can include bespoke lighting schemes, smart home integrations, specialised storage solutions (e.g., pantry systems, pull-out shelves), wine coolers, or even a dedicated coffee station. Each addition contributes to the final price.

### What is the difference between a custom and a modular kitchen?
A custom kitchen is designed and built specifically for your space and preferences, offering unique dimensions, materials, and features.
