## What are the Key Design Considerations for a Holiday Let?

Designing a holiday rental requires a different approach than designing a permanent home. The goal is to create a space that is universally appealing, highly functional, and durable enough to withstand frequent guest turnover. Here are some essential design considerations:

*   **Durability and Low Maintenance:** Choose materials that are hard-wearing and easy to clean. For instance, high-quality porcelain tiles are an excellent choice for flooring throughout the apartment, offering both elegance and resilience. Opt for robust taps & fittings and shower screens that can withstand constant use.
*   **Aesthetics and Appeal:** Aim for a bright, clean, and modern aesthetic. Neutral colour palettes are generally preferred as they appeal to a broader audience. Incorporate local touches or artwork to give the apartment a sense of place without being overly specific. Focus on creating a welcoming and relaxing atmosphere.
*   **Functionality and Comfort:** Guests appreciate well-equipped spaces. A custom kitchen with modern appliances is a major draw. Ensure bathrooms are complete bathroom renovations, featuring contemporary fixtures and ample storage. Comfortable beds and seating are paramount for guest satisfaction.
*   **Outdoor Spaces:** If your apartment has a balcony or terrace, maximise its potential. Furnish it comfortably to create an inviting outdoor living area, which is highly valued by guests on the Costa del Sol.
*   **Storage Solutions:** Provide adequate storage for guests' belongings, even for short stays. Built-in wardrobes or clever storage units can make a big difference.

## How Much Does it Cost to Renovate an Apartment for Holiday Rental in Malaga?

The cost of renovating an apartment for holiday rental in Malaga can vary significantly based on the size of the property, the extent of the renovation, and the quality of materials chosen. A full home renovation on the Costa del Sol typically ranges from €400 to €800 per square metre.

Here is a breakdown of typical costs for a comprehensive renovation:

*   **Demolition and Waste Removal:** This initial phase involves removing old fixtures, flooring, and non-structural walls. Expect costs to be around €30-€50 per square metre.
*   **Structural Work (if needed):** Any changes to load-bearing walls or significant layout alterations will incur higher costs, potentially €100-€250 per square metre, plus engineering fees.
*   **Plumbing and Electrical:** Updating these essential systems is crucial for safety and functionality. Costs can range from €80-€150 per square metre, depending on the number of new points and complexity.
*   **Flooring:** Installing new flooring, such as high-quality porcelain tiles in Málaga, can cost €30-€80 per square metre for materials and installation.
*   **Kitchen Renovation:** A custom kitchen can range from €5,000 to €15,000 or more, depending on size, appliances, and finishes. This includes cabinetry, countertops, and installation.
*   **Bathroom Renovation:** A complete bathroom renovation typically costs between €3,000 and €8,000 per bathroom, covering new fixtures, tiling, and installation.
*   **Painting and Plastering:** Refreshing walls and ceilings can cost €15-€30 per square metre.
*   **Doors and Windows:** Replacing old doors and windows improves aesthetics, insulation, and security. Costs vary widely but budget €200-€600 per door and €400-€1,000+ per window.
*   **Permits and Fees:** Factor in local council fees for renovation permits, which can be a few hundred to over a thousand euros depending on the scope.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations for Holiday Rentals in Andalusia

Before you can legally rent out your renovated apartment for short-term stays in Malaga, you must comply with the regulations set by the Junta de Andalucía. The primary requirement is to register your property as a "Vivienda con Fines Turísticos" (VFT), or Tourist Dwelling. This involves meeting specific criteria related to habitability, equipment, and services.

Key requirements often include:

*   **Habitation License (Licencia de Primera Ocupación):** Your property must have this license, confirming it meets basic habitability standards.
*   **Air Conditioning/Heating:** Properties must have air conditioning in bedrooms and living rooms if rented between May and September, and heating if rented between October and April.
*   **First Aid Kit:** A basic first aid kit must be available.
*   **Complaint Book:** An official complaint book (hoja de quejas y reclamaciones) must be provided.
*   **Tourist Registration:** Registering guests with the police (Guardia Civil or Policía Nacional) is mandatory.

It is essential to consult the official regulations from the Junta de Andalucía's Department of Tourism or seek advice from a local legal expert to ensure full compliance. Failing to register or meet the requirements can lead to significant fines.
