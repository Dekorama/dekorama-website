## Navigating Permits and Regulations for Your Mijas Renovation
Before purchasing materials, it is crucial to understand the local regulations regarding renovations in Mijas. Minor cosmetic changes typically do not require extensive permits. However, any structural alterations, changes to the exterior facade, or significant modifications to plumbing or electrical systems will likely require a building permit (licencia de obra) from the Mijas Town Hall.

Failing to obtain the necessary permits can lead to fines, delays, or even the forced demolition of unauthorised work. It is always advisable to consult with a local architect or your renovation company, such as Dekorama, who can guide you through the process. They can help ensure your project complies with Spanish building codes and local planning regulations. For general information on Spanish building regulations, you can refer to the Código Técnico de la Edificación (CTE) published by the Spanish Ministry of Public Works.
