## What Does a Full Apartment Renovation Involve?
A full apartment renovation, or reforma integral, is a comprehensive project that typically touches every aspect of your living space. It goes beyond cosmetic changes, often involving significant structural or layout modifications. Understanding the scope helps in planning and budgeting effectively.

### Key Stages of a Renovation
1.  **Planning and Design**: This initial phase involves detailed consultations with architects or designers to finalise the layout, material choices, and overall aesthetic. This is where you decide on everything from new kitchen designs to the type of porcelain tiles in Málaga you wish to use.
2.  **Permits and Licences**: Before any physical work can begin, you must secure the necessary building permits from the local council. This process can take several weeks, so it is important to factor it into your timeline.
3.  **Demolition**: Old fixtures, non-load-bearing walls, flooring, and outdated installations are removed to prepare the space for its new design.
4.  **Structural Work (if applicable)**: Any changes to load-bearing walls or significant structural modifications are carried out at this stage, always under the supervision of a qualified architect.
5.  **New Installations**: This includes updating or installing new electrical wiring, plumbing systems, and potentially new air conditioning or heating units. This is a critical stage for the long-term functionality of your home.
6.  **Wall and Ceiling Preparation**: New walls are constructed, existing ones are repaired, and ceilings are prepared for painting or other finishes.
7.  **Flooring and Tiling**: Installation of new flooring, such as wood, laminate, or ceramic and porcelain tiles, begins once the subfloor is ready.
8.  **Kitchen and Bathroom Installation**: This is where your vision for a custom kitchen or complete bathroom renovations comes to life, with the fitting of cabinets, appliances, sanitaryware, and taps.
9.  **Interior Finishes**: Painting, wallpapering, installing interior doors, and fitting built-in wardrobes are completed.
10. **Final Touches**: Lighting fixtures, skirting boards, exterior carpentry, and other decorative elements are added. The final cleaning and inspection complete the project.

## How Much Does a Full Apartment Renovation Cost in Fuengirola?
The cost of a full apartment renovation in Fuengirola varies significantly based on several factors, making it challenging to provide an exact figure without a detailed project assessment. However, we can offer a realistic range and outline the key cost drivers.

### Cost Factors to Consider
*   **Size of the Apartment**: Larger apartments naturally require more materials and labour.
*   **Quality of Materials**: Choosing premium materials like high-end porcelain tiles, custom-made kitchen cabinetry, or designer taps will increase costs compared to standard options.
*   **Scope of Work**: A renovation involving extensive layout changes, structural work, or complete overhauls of electrical and plumbing systems will be more expensive than one focused primarily on cosmetic updates.
*   **Age of the Property**: Older properties often require more extensive upgrades to infrastructure (electrical, plumbing), which adds to the overall cost.
*   **Permits and Fees**: Local council permits and technical project fees from architects or engineers are mandatory expenses.
*   **Accessibility**: If the apartment is difficult to access, requiring special equipment or extended transport of materials, costs may increase.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Fuengirola
For expats and international property owners, understanding the local regulations for renovations in Fuengirola is essential. In Spain, any significant renovation work requires a municipal licence (licencia de obra) from the local town hall (Ayuntamiento).

There are generally two types of licences:

*   **Licencia de Obra Menor (Minor Works Permit)**: For smaller, non-structural renovations like painting, changing flooring, or updating bathrooms without altering the layout. These are simpler and quicker to obtain.
*   **Licencia de Obra Mayor (Major Works Permit)**: Required for full apartment renovations that involve structural changes, alterations to the building's facade, changes in layout, or significant modifications to electrical and plumbing systems. This typically requires a technical project signed by an architect or technical engineer.

It is crucial to work with a renovation company that has a strong understanding of local regulations in Fuengirola and can assist you with the permit application process. Failing to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines, work stoppages, and even the obligation to reverse the changes. Always consult the Fuengirola Town Hall website or a local expert for the most current requirements.

### Do I need an architect for a full apartment renovation in Fuengirola?
Yes, for a full apartment renovation involving structural changes, layout modifications, or significant alterations to electrical and plumbing systems, a technical project signed by a qualified architect or technical engineer is legally required to obtain a Major Works Permit from the Fuengirola Town Hall.

### Can I live in my apartment during a full renovation?
It is generally not advisable to live in your apartment during a full renovation, especially if it involves extensive demolition, dust, noise, and disruption to essential services like water and electricity. Most clients arrange alternative accommodation for the duration of the project.
