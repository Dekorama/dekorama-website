## What Does a Full Home Renovation in Nerja Involve?
A full home renovation is a complex undertaking that touches almost every aspect of your property. It goes beyond cosmetic changes, often involving significant structural and utility work. Here is a breakdown of what you can expect:

### Design and Planning
This initial phase is critical. It involves detailed discussions about your vision, budget, and timeline. An architect or interior designer will create plans, layouts, and material specifications. This stage also includes obtaining necessary permits from the local town hall in Nerja, which your chosen renovation company should manage.

### Demolition and Structural Work
Once plans are approved, the demolition begins. This might involve removing non-load-bearing walls to create open-plan spaces, stripping out old kitchens and bathrooms, and preparing the site for new installations. Structural modifications, such as adding new openings or reinforcing existing structures, would also occur at this stage.

### Utility Updates
This is where the hidden work happens. Old electrical wiring is replaced with modern, compliant systems. Plumbing is updated, often rerouted, to accommodate new bathroom and kitchen layouts. Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems are installed or upgraded. These updates are vital for the safety and functionality of your renovated home.

### Finishes and Fittings
This is the stage where your home truly starts to take shape. It includes:

*   **Flooring:** Installation of new flooring, such as [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos), wood, or microcement.
*   **Walls and Ceilings:** Plastering, painting, and decorative finishes.
*   **Joinery:** Installation of new doors, windows, fitted wardrobes, and skirting boards.
*   **Kitchens:** The installation of [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida), including cabinetry, countertops, appliances, and splashbacks.
*   **Bathrooms:** Complete installation of [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos), including sanitaryware, tiling, and shower screens.
*   **Lighting:** Installation of all light fixtures and smart home systems.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Nerja
Any significant renovation in Spain requires municipal permits, known as `licencia de obra`. In Nerja, these are obtained from the `Ayuntamiento de Nerja` (Town Hall). The type of permit depends on the scope of work:

*   **Licencia de Obra Menor (Minor Works Permit):** For smaller, non-structural changes like updating a bathroom or kitchen, or changing flooring.
*   **Licencia de Obra Mayor (Major Works Permit):** Required for structural modifications, extensions, changes to the property's footprint, or comprehensive overhauls. This typically requires an architect's project.

Your chosen renovation company should have a deep understanding of these requirements and handle the entire permit application process on your behalf. This saves you considerable time and ensures compliance with local planning laws. You can find more general information about the local administration on the Nerja Town Hall website: [https://www.nerja.es/](https://www.nerja.es/)
