## What Factors Influence the Cost of a Custom Kitchen in Fuengirola?
The price of a custom kitchen is not a fixed figure. It is a dynamic sum influenced by several key elements. Each choice you make, from materials to appliances, contributes to the final investment.

### Materials
The selection of materials for your cabinets, countertops, flooring, and splashbacks significantly impacts the overall cost. High-end options like natural stone countertops (granite, marble, quartz), solid wood cabinetry, or designer [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos-malaga) will naturally be more expensive than laminate or standard ceramic alternatives. Durability, aesthetic appeal, and maintenance requirements also vary with material choice.

### Appliances
Integrated, high-specification appliances from premium brands will increase the budget. Think about induction hobs, built-in ovens, smart refrigerators, and wine coolers. Opting for standard, freestanding appliances can offer savings, but may not provide the seamless look often desired in a custom kitchen. Energy efficiency ratings are also a factor, as higher-rated appliances can save money in the long run.

### Size and Layout
A larger kitchen naturally requires more materials and labour. The layout also plays a role. A simple galley kitchen will be less costly than a complex U-shaped or island kitchen, which demands more cabinetry, countertop space, and potentially more intricate plumbing and electrical work. Open-plan designs, popular on the Costa del Sol, might also involve structural changes, adding to the expense.

### Design Complexity and Customisation
True custom kitchens are designed to your exact specifications. This can include bespoke cabinet sizes, unique storage solutions, intricate joinery, or specific lighting schemes. The more unique and complex the design, the more time and skill are required, influencing the price. Simple, functional designs are more budget-friendly than elaborate, highly personalised arrangements.

### Installation and Labour
Professional installation is essential for a custom kitchen. Labour costs include demolition of the old kitchen, preparation of the space, plumbing, electrical work, tiling, cabinet fitting, and appliance installation. The complexity of the installation and the experience of the renovation team will affect this portion of the budget. ## How Long Does a Custom Kitchen Renovation Take in Fuengirola?
The timeline for a custom kitchen renovation in Fuengirola can vary based on the project's scope and complexity. While every project is unique, here is a general breakdown of the stages involved.

### Planning and Design
This initial phase typically takes 2 to 4 weeks. It involves consultations, site visits, detailed measurements, 3D design creation, material selection, and finalising the layout. Clear communication with your design team is key during this stage to ensure all your requirements are met.

### Material Sourcing
Once the design is approved, ordering and receiving materials can take 3 to 6 weeks, especially for custom-made cabinetry, specific countertop materials, or imported [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium). Lead times can fluctuate, so early planning is beneficial.

### Installation
The physical installation phase usually lasts 3 to 5 weeks. This includes demolition, any necessary structural work, plumbing and electrical rough-ins, flooring, cabinet installation, countertop fitting, appliance integration, and final finishing touches. A well-coordinated team minimises disruption and ensures efficiency.

### Overall Timeline
From the initial design meeting to the final handover, most custom kitchen renovations in Fuengirola are completed within 6 to 12 weeks. Unexpected issues, such as unforeseen structural problems or delays in material delivery, can extend this timeline. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a kitchen renovation in Fuengirola?
Yes, most kitchen renovations in Fuengirola, especially those involving changes to plumbing, electrical systems, or structural elements, require a municipal building permit (licencia de obra). Minor cosmetic changes might not, but it is always best to consult with your renovation company or the local town hall to ensure compliance.

### What's the difference between a modular and a custom kitchen?
A modular kitchen uses pre-fabricated units that are assembled to fit your space, offering less flexibility in size and design. A custom kitchen is designed and built from scratch to your exact specifications, allowing for unique dimensions, materials, and features that perfectly match your home and lifestyle.

### Can I choose my own appliances?
Absolutely. In a custom kitchen project, you have full control over appliance selection. You can specify brands, models, and features that meet your culinary needs and aesthetic preferences. Your renovation company will then integrate these choices seamlessly into the overall kitchen design and installation plan.
