## How long does a full flat renovation take?
The duration of a full flat renovation in Nerja depends on the project's complexity and size, but a typical timeline for a comprehensive overhaul ranges from 8 to 16 weeks. This estimate includes various stages, from initial planning to final touches.

### Renovation timeline breakdown
Understanding the different phases helps manage expectations and plan accordingly:

*   **Planning and Design (2-4 weeks)**: This initial stage involves consultations, architectural drawings, material selection, and finalising the project scope. Securing all necessary permits also happens here.
*   **Demolition and Structural Work (1-2 weeks)**: Old fixtures are removed, walls are demolished or built, and any structural reinforcements are carried out.
*   **Installations (3-5 weeks)**: This is where new electrical wiring, plumbing systems, and HVAC installations are put in place. It is a critical phase for the functionality of your renovated flat.
*   **Finishes (4-6 weeks)**: This stage includes plastering, tiling (floors and walls), painting, and installing new doors and windows. The aesthetic transformation truly takes shape here.
*   **Kitchen and Bathroom Installation (2-3 weeks)**: Fitting custom kitchens and complete bathroom renovations, including cabinetry, appliances, sanitary ware, and taps.
*   **Final Touches and Clean-up (1 week)**: Minor adjustments, final painting, lighting installation, and a thorough clean-up before handover.

Delays can occur due to unforeseen structural issues, material delivery times, or permit processing. Working with an experienced company like Dekorama, which specialises in full home renovations on the Costa del Sol, helps minimise these risks through effective project management.

## Navigating permits and regulations in Nerja
Undertaking a full flat renovation in Nerja requires adherence to local building regulations and the acquisition of specific permits. This is a crucial step that often causes concern for international property owners, but a reputable renovation company can manage this process for you.

For a full renovation that involves structural changes, significant layout modifications, or alterations to external elements, you will typically need a *Licencia de Obra Mayor* (Major Works Permit) from the Ayuntamiento de Nerja (Nerja Town Hall). This permit ensures your project complies with urban planning laws, safety standards, and environmental regulations.

The application process usually requires detailed architectural plans, a technical project report signed by a qualified architect or technical engineer, and payment of municipal fees. Attempting a major renovation without the correct permits can lead to fines, work stoppages, and even the obligation to reverse the changes. Dekorama's team has extensive experience with local regulations across the Costa del Sol, including renovations in Benalmádena, renovations in Marbella, and renovations in Fuengirola, ensuring all paperwork is handled correctly.

For minor cosmetic updates that do not affect structure or layout, a *Licencia de Obra Menor* (Minor Works Permit) might suffice, or in some cases, no permit is needed at all. Always confirm the specific requirements for your project with the local authorities or your chosen renovation company.
