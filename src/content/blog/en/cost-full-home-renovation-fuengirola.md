## What Factors Influence the Cost of a Full Renovation in Fuengirola?
The cost of a full home renovation is not a fixed figure. It is a dynamic sum influenced by several key elements. Understanding these will help you create a realistic budget and avoid unexpected expenses.

### Property Size and Layout
Naturally, a larger property will incur higher costs due to more materials and labour required. The existing layout also plays a role. If you plan significant structural changes, such as moving walls or altering the floor plan, expect higher expenses for architectural plans, structural engineering, and increased labour.

### Scope of Work
This is perhaps the biggest cost driver. A cosmetic renovation involving new paint, flooring, and minor updates will be far less expensive than a complete overhaul that includes new plumbing, electrical systems, roof repairs, or extensions. A full home renovation often encompasses:

*   Demolition and structural modifications
*   New electrical wiring and plumbing systems
*   Installation of new flooring, such as high-quality [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos-malaga)
*   Complete [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida) and [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos)
*   New windows and doors
*   Plastering, painting, and decorative finishes
*   Air conditioning installation

### Material Choices
The quality and type of materials you select have a profound impact on the final price. Opting for [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) like natural stone, designer taps, bespoke cabinetry, or high-end appliances will increase your budget considerably compared to standard options. Dekorama offers a wide range of materials to suit various styles and budgets, ensuring quality regardless of your choice.

### Labour Costs
Skilled tradespeople are essential for a quality renovation. Labour costs include not only the workers on site but also project management, architects, and designers. Reputable companies with experienced teams typically charge more, but they also deliver higher quality work and greater peace of mind.

### Permits and Fees
In Spain, significant renovations require permits from the local council, the *Ayuntamiento*. These permits, known as *Licencia de Obra Mayor* for major works, involve fees and often require plans signed by a qualified architect. Minor works may only need a *Licencia de Obra Menor*. Neglecting permits can lead to fines and delays, so always factor these administrative costs into your budget.

### Unexpected Issues
Older properties, common in areas like Fuengirola, often hide unforeseen problems such as outdated wiring, corroded pipes, damp, or structural weaknesses. It is wise to allocate a contingency fund, typically 10-15% of your total budget, to cover these potential surprises.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Fuengirola
Understanding the local bureaucracy is crucial for any renovation project in Spain. For significant renovations in Fuengirola, you will need to apply for a *Licencia de Obra Mayor* from the *Ayuntamiento de Fuengirola*. This permit is required for structural changes, alterations to the facade, or any work that affects the property's volume or use.

The process typically involves submitting architectural plans, a project description, and various technical documents, often requiring the signature of a colegiado architect. For minor cosmetic changes, a *Licencia de Obra Menor* may suffice, which is a simpler and quicker process. Always consult with your chosen renovation company or an architect to ensure all necessary permits are secured before work begins. This avoids potential fines and ensures your renovation complies with local planning laws.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an architect for a full renovation in Fuengirola?
Yes, if your renovation involves structural changes, altering the property's volume, or changing its use, you will need an architect to draw up plans and secure a *Licencia de Obra Mayor* from the *Ayuntamiento de Fuengirola*. For minor cosmetic updates, an architect may not be necessary.

### How can I save money on my renovation?
To save money, prioritise essential upgrades, choose mid-range materials instead of luxury options where possible, and consider doing some minor tasks yourself if you have the skills. Always get multiple quotes and stick to your budget to avoid overspending.

### What is the typical payment structure for a renovation?
Most renovation companies require an initial deposit, usually 20-30% of the total cost, to begin work. Subsequent payments are then made in stages as specific project milestones are completed, with a final payment upon satisfactory completion of the entire renovation.
