## How Much Does a Complete Bathroom Renovation Cost in Manilva?
The cost of a complete bathroom renovation in Manilva varies significantly based on several key factors. These include the size of your bathroom, the quality of materials chosen, the complexity of the design, and the extent of structural or plumbing changes required. For planning purposes in 2026, it is wise to consider these current estimates as a baseline.

### Basic Renovation
For a basic renovation, expect to pay approximately €6,000 to €8,500. This typically involves replacing existing fixtures with standard, functional options, basic ceramic tiles, and minimal changes to the layout or plumbing. The focus here is on refreshing the look and functionality without extensive customisation.

### Mid-Range Renovation
A mid-range renovation usually falls between €8,500 and €12,000. This budget allows for higher quality porcelain tiles, modern taps and fittings, a new shower tray and screen, and potentially minor layout adjustments. You can achieve a stylish and durable finish with good quality materials that offer better longevity and aesthetic appeal.

### Luxury Renovation
For a luxury bathroom renovation, expect costs to start from €12,000 and go upwards of €15,000, or even €20,000+ for very large or highly customised projects. This tier includes premium materials such as large format porcelain tiles, designer sanitary ware, smart home integration, bespoke cabinetry, underfloor heating, and complex lighting schemes. Significant layout changes, including moving plumbing or electrical points, are also common in luxury projects.

## What Factors Influence the Price of a Bathroom Renovation?
Understanding the components that make up your renovation quote is essential for managing your budget. Here are the primary factors influencing the final price:

### 1. Size and Layout of the Bathroom
Larger bathrooms naturally require more materials and labour, increasing costs. If you plan to alter the layout significantly, such as moving the toilet or shower, this involves extensive plumbing work, which is a major cost driver. Keeping the existing layout can save you money.

### 2. Choice of Materials
This is often the biggest variable. The price difference between standard ceramic tiles and high-end porcelain tiles in Málaga can be substantial. Similarly, designer taps and fittings, custom shower screens, and premium sanitary ware will elevate the overall cost. Dekorama offers a wide range of premium materials to suit various budgets and styles.

### 3. Labour Costs
Skilled tradespeople are crucial for a quality finish. Labour costs include demolition, plumbing, electrical work, tiling, plastering, and installation. These costs vary based on the complexity of the job and the experience of the renovation team. Choosing an experienced company like Dekorama ensures efficient and high-quality workmanship.

### 4. Permits and Licenses
In Manilva, as with other municipalities on the Costa del Sol, you will need a building permit (licencia de obra) for most complete bathroom renovations. This is typically a *licencia de obra menor* (minor works permit) if no structural changes are made. The cost of these permits varies but generally ranges from a few hundred euros to a percentage of the project's estimated budget. Always ensure your contractor handles the necessary paperwork to avoid fines and ensure compliance with local regulations. You can find general information on building permits and regulations through your local Ayuntamiento or by consulting official Spanish government construction guidelines.

### 5. Design and Project Management
Engaging an interior designer or a renovation company that offers design services adds value by ensuring a cohesive and functional space. A professional project manager, often included in comprehensive renovation packages, oversees the entire process, coordinates trades, and manages timelines, preventing costly delays and errors.

## Choosing the Right Materials: Impact on Your Budget
The materials you select have a direct and significant impact on your bathroom renovation budget. Making informed choices can help you achieve your desired aesthetic without overspending.

### Tiles
*   **Ceramic Tiles:** More affordable, suitable for walls, good range of designs.
*   **Porcelain Tiles:** More durable, water-resistant, ideal for floors and high-traffic areas. Available in various finishes, including wood and stone effects. Dekorama specialises in high-quality porcelain tiles.
*   **Natural Stone:** Marble, granite, or travertine offer a luxurious look but come at a higher price and require more maintenance.

### Sanitary Ware and Fittings
*   **Toilets and Sinks:** Options range from basic models to wall-hung designs and integrated units with varying price points. Consider water-saving features.
*   **Taps and Shower Heads:** Chrome is standard and affordable. Black, brushed gold, or matte finishes are popular but often cost more. Quality taps and fittings from reputable brands offer better durability.
*   **Shower Trays and Screens:** Acrylic trays are economical, while resin or stone composite trays are more durable and aesthetically pleasing. Shower screens can be fixed panels, sliding, or hinged, with frameless options being more expensive.

### Lighting and Accessories
Good lighting is essential. LED spotlights are energy-efficient and popular. Consider integrated mirror lighting or decorative fixtures. Accessories like towel rails, toilet roll holders, and shelving also add to the final cost but contribute to the overall functionality and style.

## How Long Does a Bathroom Renovation Take in Manilva?
The timeline for a complete bathroom renovation in Manilva typically ranges from 2 to 4 weeks. However, this can extend depending on the project's complexity and any unforeseen issues.

### Typical Timeline Breakdown:
*   **Planning and Design (1-2 weeks):** Initial consultations, material selection, design finalisation, and permit applications.
*   **Demolition (1-2 days):** Removal of old fixtures, tiles, and sanitary ware.
*   **First Fix (1 week):** Plumbing and electrical rough-ins, any structural alterations, plastering.
*   **Tiling (1 week):** Installation of wall and floor tiles, grouting.
*   **Second Fix and Installation (3-5 days):** Installation of sanitary ware, taps, shower screen, lighting, and accessories.
*   **Finishing Touches (1-2 days):** Painting, sealing, and final cleaning.

Factors that can extend the timeline include custom orders for materials, significant layout changes requiring extensive plumbing or electrical work, and delays in permit approvals. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a bathroom renovation in Manilva?
Yes, most complete bathroom renovations in Manilva require a *licencia de obra menor* (minor works permit). This covers changes to plumbing, electrical, and tiling. Your chosen renovation company should assist with obtaining the necessary permits from the local Ayuntamiento to ensure compliance and avoid potential issues.

### How can I save money on my bathroom renovation?
You can save money by keeping the existing layout to minimise plumbing changes, choosing mid-range materials instead of high-end options, and opting for standard fixtures. Buying materials from a reputable supplier like Dekorama can also ensure competitive pricing and quality.

### What is the average lifespan of a renovated bathroom?
A well-executed bathroom renovation using quality materials can last 15-20 years or more. Investing in durable porcelain tiles, robust plumbing, and reputable sanitary ware ensures longevity. Regular maintenance also plays a crucial role in extending the life of your renovated space.

### Can I renovate my bathroom myself to save costs?
While DIY can save on labour, a complete bathroom renovation involves complex plumbing, electrical, and tiling work that requires professional expertise. Mistakes can be costly and unsafe. For a high-quality, compliant, and durable finish, hiring experienced professionals is highly recommended.
