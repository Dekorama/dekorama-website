## What Does a Full Apartment Renovation in Manilva Involve?
A 'reforma integral' means a complete overhaul of your apartment. It goes beyond cosmetic changes, addressing the core structure and systems to deliver a truly transformed living space. This comprehensive approach ensures longevity, efficiency, and a fresh aesthetic for your property in Manilva.

### Structural Changes and Layout Optimisation
This can include reconfiguring rooms, removing non-load-bearing walls to create open-plan living areas, or adding new partitions. Optimising the layout can significantly improve flow, natural light, and functionality, making your apartment feel larger and more modern. Careful planning is essential to ensure structural integrity and compliance with local building codes.

### Modernising Systems: Electrical and Plumbing
Older properties in Manilva often have outdated electrical and plumbing systems. A full renovation involves replacing old wiring, fuse boxes, pipes, and drainage. This upgrade is crucial for safety, efficiency, and accommodating modern appliances. It also prevents future costly repairs and ensures your home meets current Spanish regulations.

### Finishes and Aesthetics: Floors, Walls, and Fixtures
This is where your apartment's new look truly comes to life. It includes installing new flooring, such as high-quality [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos), replastering and painting walls, and fitting new doors and windows. Selecting contemporary fixtures, lighting, and [premium materials](/en/materiales-premium) for your kitchen and bathrooms completes the transformation, reflecting your personal style and enhancing property value.

## How Much Does a Full Apartment Renovation Cost in Manilva?
Understanding the financial commitment is paramount before starting any renovation. The cost of a full apartment renovation in Manilva varies widely based on several factors, making it difficult to give an exact figure without a detailed project assessment. However, we can provide a general guide.

### Key Cost Factors
*   **Size of the apartment**: Larger apartments naturally require more materials and labour.
*   **Quality of materials**: Choosing standard, mid-range, or luxury finishes significantly impacts the budget. For example, high-end [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos) will cost more than basic ceramic options.
*   **Scope of work**: Extensive structural changes, moving kitchens or bathrooms, and installing complex smart home systems increase costs.
*   **Permits and fees**: Local council fees for building permits (*licencia de obra*) are mandatory and vary.
*   **Professional fees**: Architects, designers, and project managers add to the overall expense.

### Budgeting for Unexpected Expenses
Always allocate an additional 10-15% of your total budget for unforeseen issues. During renovations, especially in older properties, unexpected problems like hidden damp, outdated wiring, or structural surprises can arise. Having a contingency fund prevents delays and financial stress.

### Cost per Square Metre Guide (Manilva)
*   **Basic Renovation (functional update)**: €400 - €600 per square metre. This typically covers essential updates like new flooring, basic paint, standard bathroom and kitchen fixtures, and electrical/plumbing upgrades without major layout changes.
*   **Mid-Range Renovation (modern comfort)**: €600 - €850 per square metre. This includes higher quality materials, some layout modifications, a more modern kitchen and [complete bathroom renovations](/en/banos-completos), and improved insulation or energy efficiency measures.
*   **Luxury Renovation (high-end finishes)**: €850 - €1,000+ per square metre. Expect premium materials, bespoke designs, advanced home automation, significant layout changes, and designer fixtures for a truly custom and luxurious finish.

## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Manilva
Any significant renovation in Spain requires a building permit, known as a *licencia de obra*. For a full apartment renovation, you will typically need a *licencia de obra mayor* (major works permit) from the Manilva Town Hall (Ayuntamiento).

This permit ensures your project complies with local planning laws, safety standards, and urban regulations. The application process usually involves submitting detailed plans drawn up by an architect, along with various technical reports. It is a crucial step that can take several weeks or even months, so factor this into your project timeline.

Working with a reputable local renovation company that understands Spanish planning regulations and can manage the permit application process on your behalf is highly recommended. They will ensure all paperwork is correctly filed, avoiding potential fines or project stoppages. You can find general information on Spanish planning regulations through official government portals, such as the Ministry of Public Works and Transport (Ministerio de Fomento) website, which provides broad guidelines on urban planning and construction standards in Spain.

## How Long Does a Full Apartment Renovation Take in Manilva?
The duration of a full apartment renovation depends on its complexity, the size of the property, and the efficiency of the chosen contractor. While every project is unique, here is a general timeline breakdown:

*   **Planning and Design (4-8 weeks)**: This phase involves consultations, architectural plans, material selection, and finalising the budget.
*   **Permit Application (4-12 weeks)**: Obtaining the *licencia de obra mayor* from Manilva Town Hall can be a lengthy process. It is crucial to start this early.
*   **Demolition (1-2 weeks)**: Removing old fixtures, flooring, walls, and preparing the space for new construction.
*   **Structural Work and System Installation (4-8 weeks)**: This includes any layout changes, new electrical wiring, plumbing, and HVAC systems.
*   **Finishes (4-10 weeks)**: Installing new flooring, plastering, painting, fitting new doors and windows, and installing [custom kitchens](/en/cocinas-a-medida) and bathrooms.
*   **Final Touches and Inspections (1-2 weeks)**: Cleaning, final checks, and addressing any minor adjustments.

Overall, a full apartment renovation in Manilva typically takes between 2 to 6 months from the start of physical work, after permits are secured. Delays can occur due to unforeseen issues, material availability, or bad weather, so flexibility is always advisable.

### What is the difference between reforma integral and reforma parcial?
A 'reforma integral' is a complete overhaul of your apartment, including structural changes and system upgrades. A 'reforma parcial' focuses on specific areas or cosmetic changes, such as painting a room or updating a single bathroom, without touching major systems or layout.
