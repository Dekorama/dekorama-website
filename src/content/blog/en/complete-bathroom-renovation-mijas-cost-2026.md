## What factors influence bathroom renovation costs?
Several key elements contribute to the final price tag of your bathroom renovation in Mijas. Understanding these will help you make informed decisions and manage your budget effectively.

### Size and Layout of the Bathroom
Larger bathrooms naturally require more materials and labour, increasing costs. Changing the layout, such as moving plumbing lines for a new shower or toilet position, adds significant expense due to the extra work involved in rerouting pipes and potentially altering structural elements. Keeping the existing layout largely intact can help keep costs down.

### Quality of Materials
This is often the biggest variable. From porcelain tiles to taps and fittings, the choice between budget-friendly options and premium materials can drastically alter the total cost. For example, opting for large format [porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos) from a specialist supplier will cost more than standard ceramic tiles. Similarly, designer taps or custom-made shower screens will be pricier than off-the-shelf alternatives. Dekorama offers a wide range of premium materials to suit various budgets and styles.

### Scope of Work
Are you simply replacing fixtures and refreshing surfaces, or are you undertaking a complete overhaul? A full home renovation often includes gutting the entire space, which involves demolition, waste removal, new electrical wiring, plumbing, waterproofing, and then installation. Each additional task contributes to the overall labour and material costs.

### Labour Costs
Skilled tradespeople, including plumbers, electricians, tilers, and general builders, command professional rates. The complexity and duration of the work directly impact labour expenses. Choosing experienced professionals ensures quality and compliance with local regulations, which is a worthwhile investment.

### Unexpected Issues
Older properties on the Costa del Sol, especially in areas like Mijas, can hide unforeseen problems such as damp, outdated wiring, or corroded pipes. These issues, discovered during demolition, require immediate attention and can add to the budget and timeline. It is wise to allocate a contingency fund, typically 10-15% of the total estimated cost, for such eventualities.

## Permits and regulations for bathroom renovations in Mijas
Navigating local regulations is an essential part of any renovation project in Spain. For a complete bathroom renovation in Mijas, you will likely need a municipal permit, known as a "Licencia de Obra Menor" (minor works permit) or potentially a "Licencia de Obra Mayor" (major works permit) if structural changes are involved.

The specific requirements and fees vary by municipality. It is crucial to check with the Mijas Town Hall (Ayuntamiento de Mijas) directly to understand the exact permits needed for your project. Failing to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines and potential delays. A reputable renovation company like Dekorama will assist you with this process, ensuring all necessary paperwork is filed correctly. You can find more information on local government services via the [Mijas Town Hall website](https://www.mijas.es/portal/es/tramites-y-gestiones/urbanismo-y-vivienda).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a bathroom renovation in Mijas?
Yes, for most complete bathroom renovations in Mijas, you will need a "Licencia de Obra Menor" from the Mijas Town Hall. If structural changes are involved, a "Licencia de Obra Mayor" might be required. Always check local regulations before starting work to avoid issues.

### How can I save money on my bathroom renovation?
To save money, consider keeping the existing layout to avoid plumbing changes. Choose mid-range materials instead of high-end options. You can also opt for standard-sized fixtures and tiles. Doing some of the simpler tasks yourself, like painting, can also reduce labour costs.

### What is the typical warranty for bathroom renovation work?
Reputable renovation companies typically offer a warranty on their workmanship, often ranging from 1 to 3 years. Materials usually come with their own manufacturer's warranties. Always clarify the warranty terms with your chosen contractor before signing any agreement.
