## Navigating Permits and Regulations for Renovations in Spain

Undertaking a renovation in Manilva, like anywhere in Spain, often requires municipal permits. The type of permit needed depends on the scope of your work. Minor renovations (obra menor) might only require a simple notification, while major structural changes or extensions (obra mayor) demand a more extensive application process, including architectural plans.

It is essential to consult the local *Ayuntamiento* (town hall) in Manilva or a legal professional specialising in Spanish property law to understand the specific requirements for your project. Failing to obtain the correct permits can lead to fines or even the demolition of unauthorised work. Reputable renovation companies often assist with this process, simplifying the administrative burden for expats. For general information on building permits in Spain, you can consult official government resources such as the [administracion.gob.es portal](https://administracion.gob.es/pag_Home/Tu-Administracion/Ciudadanos/Vivienda/Permisos-obras.html) (in Spanish).
