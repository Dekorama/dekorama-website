The range of materials available for renovation is vast, each with specific applications and benefits. For properties in Torremolinos and the Costa del Sol, certain materials are particularly well-suited due to their performance and aesthetic qualities.

Flooring is a foundational element that dictates the feel and practicality of any space. [Porcelain tiles in Málaga](/en/porcelanicos) are an excellent choice for the region. These areas demand materials that combine hygiene, durability, and style. ### Outdoor materials
Given the outdoor lifestyle on the Costa del Sol, exterior materials are just as important. Anti-slip porcelain tiles, natural stone paving, and durable cladding for facades are popular choices. These materials must withstand direct sunlight, rain, and temperature fluctuations without fading or deteriorating.

### Lighting
Beyond functional illumination, lighting fixtures contribute significantly to the ambiance and design of a space. Consider energy-efficient LED solutions, both for interior and exterior use, to enhance aesthetics and reduce utility costs.
