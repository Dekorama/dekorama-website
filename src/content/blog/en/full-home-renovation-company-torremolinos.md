## Navigating Permits and Regulations in Torremolinos
One of the most critical aspects of any renovation in Spain is obtaining the correct permits. Ignoring this step can lead to fines, forced demolition, or legal issues. In Torremolinos, like other municipalities on the Costa del Sol, you will typically need a 'Licencia de Obra Mayor' (Major Works Permit) for a full home renovation involving structural changes or significant alterations to the property's layout or exterior.

This permit requires submission of architectural plans and technical reports, often signed by a qualified architect or technical architect. The process can be time-consuming, sometimes taking several months for approval. A reputable renovation company will have an in-house team or established relationships with local architects and technical experts to manage this entire process on your behalf, ensuring all documentation is correctly prepared and submitted to the Torremolinos Town Hall.

They will also advise on any specific local regulations, such as those concerning facade changes in historic areas or restrictions on certain types of materials. This expert guidance is invaluable for expats, ensuring your project complies with all Spanish laws and local ordinances.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an architect for my renovation?
For a full home renovation involving structural changes or significant layout alterations, yes, an architect or technical architect is legally required in Spain to design the project and oversee the works. Your renovation company will typically include this service or work with trusted professionals.

### How long does a full renovation typically take?
The duration varies greatly depending on the property's size and the renovation's complexity. A small apartment might take 3-5 months, while a larger villa could take 6-9 months or more. Your chosen company will provide a detailed timeline.

### Can I live in my property during the renovation?
For a full home renovation, it is generally not advisable or practical to live in the property. The work involves significant disruption, dust, noise, and often the disconnection of essential services like water and electricity. Temporary relocation is usually necessary.

### What kind of warranty should I expect?
Reputable renovation companies in Spain typically offer a warranty on their workmanship, often for one to three years, and material warranties as provided by manufacturers. Always ensure your contract clearly states the warranty terms for both labour and materials.
