## What Factors Influence Your Bathroom Renovation Budget?
Several key factors directly impact the final cost of your complete bathroom renovation. Understanding these elements allows you to make informed decisions and manage your budget effectively.

### Material Selection
Materials represent a significant portion of your budget. The choices you make for surfaces, fixtures, and fittings will dictate both the aesthetic and the overall expense.

*   **Tiles**: From standard ceramic to high-end porcelain tiles, prices vary considerably. Dekorama offers a wide range of porcelain tiles in Málaga, suitable for all budgets and styles.
*   **Sanitaryware**: Toilets, sinks, and bidets come in various designs and qualities. Wall-hung options are often more expensive due to complex installation.
*   **Taps & Fittings**: Designer taps and fittings can elevate the look but also increase the cost. Consider water-saving options for long-term efficiency.
*   **Shower Trays & Screens**: Custom-made shower screens or designer shower trays will cost more than standard sizes. Walk-in showers often require more extensive waterproofing.
*   **Lighting**: Integrated LED lighting, mood lighting, and smart home integrations add to the cost but significantly enhance functionality and ambiance.
*   **Vanity Units & Storage**: Custom-built units are pricier than off-the-shelf options but offer tailored solutions and better use of space.

### Labour Costs
Labour is another major component. This includes demolition, plumbing, electrical work, tiling, plastering, painting, and installation. The complexity of the work, the number of tradespeople involved, and the duration of the project all contribute to labour expenses. Opting for a full-service renovation company simplifies coordination and often provides better value.

### Size and Layout Changes
Larger bathrooms naturally cost more to renovate due to increased material and labour requirements. Moving walls, repositioning plumbing, or altering electrical points significantly adds to the complexity and cost. If you are considering a complete overhaul, these structural changes need to be factored in early.

### Permits and Regulations
In Spain, most significant renovation projects require a building permit (licencia de obra) from the local town hall. For San Pedro de Alcantara, which falls under the Marbella municipality, you will need to apply to the Ayuntamiento de Marbella. There are usually two types: a 'licencia de obra menor' for minor works and a 'licencia de obra mayor' for structural changes. Fees apply for these permits, and it is crucial to obtain them before starting work to avoid fines. An experienced renovation company will assist with this process. You can find more information on local regulations via the Marbella Town Hall's official website.

### Do I need a permit for a bathroom renovation in San Pedro de Alcantara?
Yes, most complete bathroom renovations in San Pedro de Alcantara require a building permit (licencia de obra) from the Marbella Town Hall. ### What is the typical warranty period for renovations?
In Spain, renovation companies typically offer a warranty on their workmanship, often ranging from 1 to 3 years. Materials usually come with manufacturer warranties. Always clarify the warranty terms with your chosen contractor before signing any agreements to ensure peace of mind.

### Can I choose my own materials for the renovation?
Absolutely. While many renovation companies, including Dekorama, offer a wide selection of premium materials, you are generally free to source your own. However, discussing your choices with your contractor is advisable to ensure compatibility, quality, and to avoid potential installation issues or delays.

### How can I save money on a bathroom renovation?
To save money, consider keeping the existing layout to avoid plumbing changes, opting for mid-range rather than luxury materials, and choosing standard-sized fixtures. Buying materials during sales or working with a company that offers competitive pricing on premium materials can also help manage your budget effectively.
